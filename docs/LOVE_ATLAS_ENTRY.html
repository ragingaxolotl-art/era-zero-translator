Love

DBA compound · approved October 28, 2026

A lasting bond through which we keep awakening who we have yet to become.
LIF:210 — established life-bond
The connection that endures between living identities.
LIF:101 — awaken or activate oneself
The awakening within us that gives expression to the self.
LIF:002 — dormant self
The self preserved without present conscious expression: poetically, who we have yet to become.
num — unresolved possibility
Love remains unfinished throughout the life course. Its bond can be established while its possibilities continue to unfold.

Poetic reading: never finished becoming. Love exists, and there is always more of it to discover and become.

Translator: select love for this compound. Use love [2] or ASSERT "love" [2]. for its poetic num occurrence.

The compound centers LIF:210; LIF:101 and LIF:002 describe that bond. The three atoms retain their meanings. Num is an occurrence state, not a fourth atom or a change to its existing meaning. Lifelong becoming is the approved literary interpretation, not a literal tense encoded by num.

This word names this specific conception of love; ordinary affection or preference remains a separate translator choice.
