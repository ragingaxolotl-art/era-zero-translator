# REP:200–222 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges all nine provisional Repair capacity/relation meanings from first principles. It compares five genuinely different architectures at every address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and routes 105 English concepts without forcing broad words into atoms.

## Packet-level decision

The strongest architecture makes `REP:2xx` the restorative-affordance layer paired with canonical `REP:1xx`. `REP:200–202` says whether one reference entity has capacity for internally originating restoration. `REP:210–212` says whether one distinct restorative source and one target have an applicability relation that can enable target restoration. `REP:220–222` says whether one shared restoration condition has an enabling pathway across multiple participants in a shared system.

This replaces the Phase 2 mixture of resilience, protection, actual regeneration, maintenance responsibility, active care, observer verification, backups, adaptive processes, and mere possibility. Those candidates were useful prompts but blurred conditions, affordances, processes, resources, service relations, evidence, protection, and modality. The recommended grid uses one persistent/operative/latent contrast and remains neutral about substrate, observer knowledge, mechanics, resources, protection, scheduling, and actual realization.

### Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** persistent internal recoverability

**Invariant:** A persistent capacity of one reference entity to undergo internally originating restoration under qualifying conditions, independently of whether that capacity is presently operative.

**Boundary:** Includes only the persistent independently of current operation substrate-neutral restorative affordance for internal recoverability. The recoverability belongs to one reference entity and concerns internally originating restoration; a distinct restorative source selects the outward column. It may be realized physically, biologically, computationally, informationally, socially, or conceptually, but it does not assert that the matching REP:1xx transformation occurs, that a REP:0xx restoration condition is realized, or that mechanics, evidence, knowledge, protection, maintenance, resources, probability, temporal, or modal conditions hold.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The restoration condition, bearer, restorative source, target, participant, or shared system as an entity or realized state.
- An actual internal restoration, source-to-target restoration, or coordinated systemic restoration process at any completion phase.
- Physical force, energy, contact, compatibility, route, biological mechanism, structural dependency, communicative channel, data-flow edge, remedy, backup, spare, or caregiver as such.
- Detection, verification, diagnosis, evidence, service messages, belief, confidence, attribution, probability, expected success, or recovery score.
- Resistance, immunity, shielding, filtering, prevention, maintenance, preservation, service responsibility, replacement, or other protective or resource affordance.
- Readiness, plan, schedule, future time, permission, necessity, unrestricted possibility, or guaranteed occurrence or success of restoration.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_2`: Selects a restorative capacity or enabling relationship rather than a restoration-condition entity or an actual restorative transformation.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward scope: one reference entity and its internally originating restorative change; this root names the affordance for internal recoverability and no competing family claim.
- `third_trit`: Selects persistent independently of current operation expression of the same internal recoverability; runtime truth status, tense, probability, permission, and process phase remain separate.

**Nearest neighbors:** `REP:201`, `REP:202`, `REP:100`, `REP:101`, `REP:102`, `REP:000`

**Same-family boundary:** REP:0xx names restoration-condition entities: internal, target, and shared acceptable conditions. REP:1xx names actual restorative transformations: internal restoration, source-to-target restoration, and coordinated systemic restoration. REP:2xx names only the corresponding restorative affordance: inward recoverability, outward source-target restorative applicability, or a systemic restoration pathway. An affordance does not entail that its process begins or completes; an actual process does not entail that the affordance persists afterward. Persistent 2x0 identity may co-occur with its operative or latent sibling, while operative and latent are mutually exclusive for the same affordance at one reference time.

**Runtime meanings:**

- `[0]`: Denies that the persistent internal recoverability persists in the stated restoration context; it does not select the operative or latent sibling and does not establish a different restorative affordance.
- `[1]`: Affirms that the persistent internal recoverability persists independently of current operation, without asserting that internal, target, or systemic restoration is occurring.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether the persistent internal recoverability persists; the lexical root remains the persistent member and does not become latent.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** Lexical third trit 0 permanently selects the persistent member of this restorative-affordance triplet. Runtime `[2]/num` instead marks a token-level assertion pending, remembered, or unresolved. Thus `REP:200[1]` affirms the lexical concept, while a persistent or operative sibling carrying `[2]/num` remains that sibling and never becomes a latent root.
