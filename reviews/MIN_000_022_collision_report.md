# MIN:000–022 Collision Report

Status: **peer-review evidence — pending delegated approval**

## Core ownership rule

The proposed block owns cognitive representations as entities or conditions: self-representation, target representation, and shared cognitive schema, each persistent, manifest, or latent. It does not own attention, perception, learning, reasoning, belief commitment, truth, external records, communication, physical neural state, identity, error, or creation.

## High-risk English traps

- **Memory** may name content, retention, storage, or recall. The proposed atoms own representational content and expression, not every memory relation.
- **Awareness** may mean consciousness, attention, or a manifest representation. Only the representational sense is `MIN:001/011/021`.
- **Knowledge** adds truth or justification; a representation alone may be false.
- **Shared** requires cognitive content across cognizers. A common record, channel, target, or simultaneous gaze is insufficient.
- **Latent** is lexical nonmanifest representation. Runtime `[2]/num` marks token status and does not select the latent root.
- **Model** may be an external DAT/STR artifact or an internal cognitive representation. Bearer and use decide the family.

## Disposition

No blocking collision remains. The architecture reserves processes and capacities for later MIN blocks and leaves truth, data, communication, identity, structure, and physical realization in their own domains.
