## Required interpretation

`REP:0xx` identifies restoration conditions. `REP:1xx` identifies actual restorative transformations. `REP:2xx` states only the corresponding restorative affordance: one entity's internal recoverability, one source-target restorative-applicability relation, or one pathway that can enable one shared restoration condition across system participants. No layer entails another.

Persistent, operative, and latent lexical thirds are part of root identity. Persistent identity may co-occur with operation or latency; operative and latent are exclusive for the same affordance at one reference time. Runtime `[0]`, `[1]`, or `[2]/num` qualifies an assertion and never changes lexical status. In particular `REP:200[2]/num` is uncertainty about persistent recoverability, while `REP:202[1]` affirms latent recoverability.
