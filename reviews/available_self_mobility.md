# Available self-mobility sentence meaning

Source: current MOV:201 invariant in `lexicon/atoms.yaml`: a mover's capacity to
change its own position that is presently available for exercise.

“The robot is able to move” binds that robot as bearer of MOV:201. It asserts
available capacity, not actual motion, permission, a path, speed or locomotion
manner. “Not able to move” scopes negation over the capacity assertion; it does
not replace the occurrence's separately selected runtime 0/1/2.

This is sentence composition using an existing sense, not a new atom, compound
or reinterpretation of the root. The construction supports the existing named,
definite, deictic and counted bearer grammar. Destination, manner and transitive
complements need additional composition and are not silently dropped. Bare
“can move” still needs ability-versus-permission handling.

Six state/negation cases match Ruby/browser formal and MIDI output and retain
distinct performances. Independent strict MIDI observation recovers MOV:201,
its ending and negation frame. Questions and bearer forms are parsed separately.
This does not establish full motion-language coverage or historical approval.
