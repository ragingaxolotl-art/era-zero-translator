# MAT:100–122 Adversarial Semantic Peer Review

Status: **READY FOR USER APPROVAL — NOT CANONICAL**

Scope is strictly `MAT:100–122`. The current Phase 2 meanings are candidates, not presumptions. Deterministic generated fields are fixed. The review depends only on Core Architecture 1.0 and the 45 canonical LIF/MOV roots; it does not treat pending `MOV:200–222` or `MAT:000–022` recommendations as authority.

## Executive recommendation

Use one invariant process—**physical-state change**—with two orthogonal address dimensions:

- second trit `0`: change localized within the focal material bearer;
- second trit `1`: change localized in a distinct material target;
- second trit `2`: change distributed across materially interacting parts of a shared system;
- third trit `0/1/2`: completed / ongoing / possible-but-nonactualized.

This removes result-state stability, consolidation, fabrication purpose, force, transfer, resource roles, cycles, staging, and queues from the atomic definitions. Those meanings remain available to compounds, roles, grammar, other families, or later MAT layers.

> Completion of a physical-state change localized within the focal material bearer.

**Address fit.** Selects a process/action/transformation rather than a material entity or capacity. Localizes the physical-state change within the focal bearer: inward/self/source orientation. Selects the completed lexical phase: the change event reached its boundary.

**Boundaries.** Includes a change in an intrinsic physical state of matter at the stated observational scale. Allows thermal, phase, compositional, chemical, electrical, or mechanical specialization without building those subtypes into the atom. Does not require an intentional agent, beneficial or harmful result, transfer path, schedule, or named cause. The process locus is focal/inward and the lexical phase is completed.

**Exclusions.** Position or displacement without intrinsic physical-state change belongs to MOV. Creation purpose belongs to CRE; structural organization to STR; damage to WRN; restoration to REP. Mental, informational, logical, social, and ontological changes belong to their own families. Matter/energy transport, input, output, circulation, or exchange requires MOV/CON roles unless intrinsic physical-state change is also asserted. Scheduling, planning, queueing, readiness, probability, and future certainty are never supplied by lexical trit 2. A material entity/condition belongs to MAT:0xx; a capacity or relationship remains for MAT:2xx review.

**Nearest neighbors.** Same triplet `MAT:100–MAT:102`; same-phase other loci `MAT:110`, `MAT:120`. MAT:0xx describes material bearers/conditions, not change events. MAT:2xx remains provisional and must not be preempted by this process root.

**Runtime states.** `[0]` Denies or deactivates the claim that the focal/inward physical-state change reached completion. `[1]` Affirms that the focal/inward change reached its completion boundary; it does not describe the resulting entity state. `[2]/num` Marks the completion assertion itself as dormant, unavailable, remembered, or unresolved. It does not select lexical potential `MAT:102`.

**Lexical/runtime distinction.** The third lexical trit permanently selects completed physical-state change. Runtime `[2]/num` instead qualifies the current status of a token of that already selected lexeme and never inserts planning, scheduling, or a different lexical phase.

**Boundaries.** Includes a change in an intrinsic physical state of matter at the stated observational scale. **Exclusions.** Position or displacement without intrinsic physical-state change belongs to MOV. Creation purpose belongs to CRE; structural organization to STR; damage to WRN; restoration to REP. Scheduling, planning, queueing, readiness, probability, and future certainty are never supplied by lexical trit 2. **Nearest neighbors.** Same triplet `MAT:120–MAT:122`; same-phase other loci `MAT:102`, `MAT:112`. MAT:0xx describes material bearers/conditions, not change events. MAT:2xx remains provisional and must not be preempted by this process root.

**Lexical/runtime distinction.** The third lexical trit permanently selects potential/nonactualized physical-state change. Runtime `[2]/num` instead qualifies the current status of a token of that already selected lexeme and never inserts planning, scheduling, or a different lexical phase.
