# Discovery labels must preserve reviewed meanings

The plain-English index previously synthesized a display label and explanation
from each root's first two address trits and a generic phase label. This produced
claims such as “learning or changing an idea” for mental-model revision and
“possible or planned” for unrealized processes. Model revision need not constitute
learning; possibility does not establish a plan. A disclaimer below an overstated
label does not repair that label.

Atom display labels and explanations now use the reviewed title and core
definition. Broad search tags remain discovery aids and never authorize a sentence
translation. Compound labels and all canonical definitions, root sounds, senses
and runtime states remain unchanged.

Checking all 376 labels exposed a second defect: heuristic English tense detection
could downgrade an exact nonactualized-process title and rank a completed process
above it. Exact reviewed dictionary labels now precede heuristic matches and do
not undergo that phase guess. This preserves lexical potential independently of
the explicit occurrence runtime. Ordinary sentences still require composition;
the priority rule does not make unsupported learning, understanding or planning
sentences translatable.

Validation checks every atom's displayed title and definition against authority,
all 376 target rankings, and rejection of automatic resolution for those unsupported
full sentences. These are dictionary fidelity checks, not increased language
coverage or a claim that missing predicates have been implemented.

The integration audit also exposed a vocabulary-rebuild regression: the Gate 4C
builder reset later pipeline status fields and replaced poetic love's specific
selection cues with generic compound cues. The builder now preserves completed
pipeline status and the approved poetic cues. Before updating the general-English
fingerprint, all 458 prior senses were compared as complete records and preserved;
only the two new feeling senses were added. This retains the historical candidate
pipeline status, not a claim that all seven translator completion gates are closed.
