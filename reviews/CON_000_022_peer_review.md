# CON:000–022 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges every Phase 2 meaning in the Connection entity/condition layer. It compares five genuinely different semantic architectures per address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and permits compounds, grammar, other families, and unresolved gaps rather than forcing English words into atoms.

## Packet-level design decision

The strongest permanent architecture treats `CON:0xx` as **communicative loci**: a source endpoint, target endpoint, or shared channel, each expressed as persistent, active, or dormant. This produces one coherent 3×3 grid and reserves communication actions for `CON:1xx` and capacities, bonds, compatibility, and possibilities for `CON:2xx`.

A participant's biological identity is LIF; a device or cable is MAT; topology is STR; a message or signal is DAT; attention and understanding are MIN; protocol rules are LOG. CON owns only the endpoint or shared channel as a place of communicative participation.

### D. Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** persistent source endpoint

**Invariant:** A bounded communicative locus that remains identified as the source-side endpoint of an exchange context.

**Inclusion boundary:** Includes only a communicative locus at this exact source, target, or shared scope and this exact persistent, active, or dormant lexical expression. The bearer may be a person, device, service, port, room, session, medium, or other entity, but this root names its place in an exchange context rather than the bearer itself.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The identity, life, consciousness, location, physical composition, or structure of the endpoint bearer.
- A message, signal payload, symbol, record, protocol, language rule, or stored address as an information object.
- Sending, receiving, delivering, broadcasting, negotiating, acknowledging, or otherwise performing a communication process.
- A bond, agreement, compatibility relation, reachability capacity, permission, access right, or possible network relation.
- Online/offline runtime status, success, failure, schedule, intention, attention, comprehension, or future participation unless another root or grammar supplies it.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_0`: Selects an entity or condition: persistent source endpoint is a communicative endpoint locus, not the act of communicating and not the relationship or capacity connecting participants.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward/source scope: the originating side is the locus named by the root.
- `third_trit`: Selects stable or persistent lexical expression: the endpoint or channel is retained as a persistent identity.

**Nearest neighbors:** `CON:001`, `CON:002`, `CON:010`, `CON:020`, `CON:100`, `CON:200`, `DAT:000`, `STR:000`

**Same-family boundary:** CON:0xx owns communicative endpoint and channel loci; CON:1xx must own communication processes such as transmission or receipt; CON:2xx must own capacities, bonds, compatibility, and unrealized relational possibilities.

**Runtime-state interpretation:**

- `[0]`: Denies that the persistent identification of this source-endpoint locus currently holds in the exchange context; it does not assert either active participation or dormant retention.
- `[1]`: Affirms that this source-endpoint locus remains persistently identified across the stated exchange context, whether or not a particular exchange act is occurring.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether the persistent identification of this source-endpoint locus currently holds; it does not change source/target/shared scope or select an active or dormant sibling.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** The third lexical trit permanently selects stable or persistent endpoint/channel character. Runtime `[2]/num` separately marks the status of this token's assertion. Thus CON:000[1] affirms the persistent source endpoint lexeme, while CON:000[2]/num leaves the assertion about that same lexeme unresolved.
