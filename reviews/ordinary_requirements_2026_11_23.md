# Ordinary requirement phrases

The original audit distinguishes “must not open” (a requirement that opening not occur) from “does not have to open” (no requirement to open). The story sentence flow previously composed neither. The published grammar already defines separate modality_obligated and modality_necessary frames, so this extension adds no atoms, senses, compounds or musical frames.

After the three existing parser passes fail, a strict requirement pass reuses the need-to predicate grammar for must [not], have/has/had to [not], and do/does/did/will/might [not] have to [not]. Ordinary agreement, active verbs, supported states, ongoing aspect, descriptions, placement and maintained conditions retain their established interpretation. Earlier accepted readings, personal meanings and need-to performances have priority. The selected predicate and its role bindings do not change when selecting a requirement interpretation.

Must and have-to do not automatically establish the source of necessity. Before scoring, a plain-language choice distinguishes a rule/duty/commitment from practical/logical necessity, with another meaning left unresolved. The first selects obligated, the second necessary. The source of a rule, its urgency and fulfillment are not invented. Past and future scope the requirement; a past duty does not assert that the required event happened. A possible requirement is possibility outside the selected mode. This is a bounded modal reading, not a full account of epistemic evidence or conversational force.

Must not places negation inside the selected requirement. Does not have to places negation outside. Both ordinary questions and statements keep that order. Contracted statement mustn't expands through the existing contraction layer; inverted mustn't questions are excluded because their bias and scope need separate review. Existing need-to retains its earlier necessary mode and exact music.

The requirement choice is resolved before date or direct negative quantity choices, which retain the selected mode. Date choices distinguish when the event occurs from when the obligation applies. Supported quantified subjects keep their existing class scope and restrictions, including unresolved shared-versus-per-member indefinite items. Period-separated clauses may reuse established neutral references and choose a separate mode for each requirement. Newly required linked clauses are withheld because their relationships may fall under the modal; their scope must not silently move outside it.

Perfect must-have, should/ought, passive requirements naming an agent, additional adverbs/complements, restricted quantities and multiple-modal date attachment remain open. Stored/closed without an agent remain the existing state readings. No permission/ability alias is added. No promise, intent, actual compliance or authority source is inferred.

Validation must recover exact ordered scopes, roles, speech act and runtime 0/1/2 from melody and expanded chords without the original text. Check obligation truth counterexamples, necessity contrasts, plain-language readback, personal meanings, old performances and actual browser choices/downloads. This does not prove general recorded-audio decoding or complete unrestricted English. This author review implements the standing grammar/usability goal; it does not assert new phrase-by-phrase user approval.
