# First foundation batch: ongoing restoration

Authority: user-directed translator-completion goal, October 28, 2026.
No canonical root or existing compound is changed by this review.

These meanings come first because all ingredients already have precise approved

## Ongoing physical repair

Head REP:111, qualified by MAT:111. Definition: an ongoing source-to-target
restorative transformation whose restorative transformation is an ongoing
physical-state change localized in that distinct material target.

- Include: actively restoring a damaged physical target toward its restoration standard.
- Exclude: repair already completed; repair merely possible; arbitrary physical change;
  a promise or future intention to repair; merely having restoration capacity.
- Reuse: the graph pattern of approved physical repair, not its completed meaning.
- Contrast: “Pip is repairing the machine” versus “Pip repaired the machine.”
- Later reuse: material restoration with a tool, restoration of a living body.
  Do not label all healing with this meaning: source/target and material-change
  conditions must hold, and internal restoration is a different process center.

Alternatives considered: reuse REP:111 alone (loses the material restriction);
reuse the completed compound with runtime 1 (confuses occurrence status with lexical
completion); use the active two-root graph (retains both required distinctions).

## Ongoing internal data repair

Head REP:101, qualified by DAT:101. Definition: an ongoing restorative
transformation originating within one reference entity whose restorative
transformation is an ongoing bounded transformation of represented information
within that entity, without transfer to a distinct target.

- Include: an internal process restoring damaged represented information.
- Exclude: arbitrary editing, copying to another target, restoration already finished,
  data merely kept in reserve, and restoration caused by a distinct external source
  unless that source is represented by a separate appropriate construction.
- Reuse: the structure of the approved completed data-repair compound.
- Contrast: “The system is repairing its internal data” versus “The system is sending data.”
- Later reuse: recovery of damaged records under an explicit restoration standard.

Alternatives considered: DAT:101 alone (does not assert restoration); completed
data repair with num (does not mean ongoing repair); active restorative/data
process combination (retains domain, phase, and internal origin).

## Ongoing internal structural repair

Head REP:101, qualified by STR:101. Definition: an ongoing restorative
transformation originating within one reference structure whose restorative
transformation changes how that structure's constituents occupy its internal
positions.

- Include: internal structural reconfiguration toward the applicable restoration standard.
- Exclude: arbitrary rearrangement, incorporation of a distinct target, creation
  of a peer composite, completed restoration, and merely possible restoration.
- Reuse: the structure of approved completed structural repair.
- Contrast: “The structure is restoring its internal arrangement” versus
  “The structure is incorporating another part.”
- Later reuse: self-reorganization that restores structure; do not use it for
  every English use of “rebuild.”

Alternatives considered: STR:101 alone (restoration is absent); REP:111 plus
STR:101 (mixes outward restorative origin with internal structural origin without
an explicit relation); REP:101 plus STR:101 (aligned internal restoration).
