# WRN:000–022 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges all nine provisional Warning entity meanings. It compares five genuinely different architectures per address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and routes 92 English concepts without forcing broad words into atoms.

## Packet-level design decision

The strongest architecture makes `WRN:0xx` the substrate-neutral entity/condition layer for adversity. The inward triplet names an intrinsic adverse condition inside one reference entity, the outward triplet a distinct external hazard relative to one target, and the systemic triplet one correlated distributed adverse condition across multiple participants. Persistence, current manifestation, and dormancy supply the exact third-trit contrast.

The third-trit grid is deliberately not a three-way exclusive partition. Each `...0` member asserts persistence of adverse-condition identity and is neutral about present manifestation, so it may co-occur with either its `...1` or `...2` sibling. The `...1` and `...2` siblings are mutually exclusive for the same condition at the same reference time. Runtime `[2]/num` remains uncertainty about the selected claim rather than a lexical sibling.

The design rejects the provisional mixture of boundedness, evidence, confirmation, approach, attack, causal harm, spread, probability, and general risk. Those require DAT, MIN, CON, MOV, LOG, ORI, domain claims, or later WRN process/relation roots.

### D. Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** persistent internal fault

**Invariant:** An intrinsic adverse condition that remains identified within one reference entity independently of whether it is presently producing harmful deviation.

**Inclusion boundary:** Includes only the persistent intrinsic adverse condition as a substrate-neutral claim about one intrinsic adverse condition located within a reference entity. The claim is about an adverse condition intrinsic to the reference entity, not about an external source, an observer's belief, or a diagnostic record. It is neutral about physical mechanism, observer knowledge, evidential certainty, signaling, formal validity, moral blame, repairability, severity, probability, timing, and response.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The person, organism, material object, software component, institution, or system that bears, causes, observes, records, or responds to the condition.
- Physical fracture, force, heat, collision, displacement, approach, biological injury, illness, fear, or pain without a separate applicable WRN adverse classification.
- Formal falsehood, invalidity, contradiction, type error, permission violation, incompatibility, or schema mismatch merely as a rule judgment.
- Alarm, warning message, symptom report, log entry, error code, measurement, diagnosis, belief, suspicion, attention, or evidence about the condition.
- Detecting, warning, attacking, spreading, containing, isolating, shutting down, repairing, recovering, or responding as a process or relation.
- Severity, probability, uncertainty to an observer, possible future occurrence, schedule, cause, blame, intent, exploitability, vulnerability, exposure, or guaranteed harm.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_0`: Selects an entity or condition: persistent internal fault is an adverse condition, not its detection, warning, containment, propagation, causal mechanism, susceptibility relation, evidence record, or repair.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward/self scope: one intrinsic adverse condition located within a reference entity and no other Warning-family column.
- `third_trit`: Selects the stable/persistent condition by adverse identity or manifestation while leaving runtime truth status, observer knowledge, tense, probability, and response phase separate.

**Nearest neighbors:** `WRN:001`, `WRN:002`, `WRN:010`, `WRN:020`, `WRN:100`, `WRN:200`

**Same-family boundary:** `WRN:0xx` owns adverse conditions as entities; `WRN:1xx` remains the process layer for detecting, warning, containing, or responding; `WRN:2xx` remains the capacity/relation layer for susceptibility, exposure, and systemic risk relations. Manifestation in 0xx does not itself name the process that caused, detected, communicated, propagated, or remediated it, and an adverse condition does not entail a particular vulnerability or exposure relation. The persistent member is manifestation-neutral: it may co-occur with the manifest sibling or the dormant sibling at the same reference time. It asserts continuity of adverse-condition identity rather than a third mutually exclusive manifestation value.

**Runtime-state interpretation:**

- `[0]`: Denies that the persistent intrinsic adverse condition remains identified in the stated warning context; it does not affirm the manifest or dormant sibling and does not assert safety in every other respect.
- `[1]`: Affirms that the intrinsic adverse condition persists as identified, without deciding current harmful manifestation, evidence, awareness, severity, damage, response, probability, or causal history beyond the invariant.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether the persistent intrinsic adverse condition remains identified; the token stays the stable lexical root and does not become the manifest or dormant sibling.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** Lexical third trit `0` permanently selects the stable/persistent member of this adverse-condition triplet. Runtime `[2]/num` instead leaves the truth or applicability of that already-selected root unresolved; it never changes manifestation phase. Thus a latent root affirmed with `[1]` is a confirmed latent condition, while a persistent or manifest root carrying `[2]/num` remains lexically persistent or manifest but uncertain as a token claim.
