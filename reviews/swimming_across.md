# Ongoing swimming across a region

Convention 22 reuses exact swimming definition 1.0.0 and adds across-traversal
of a bound spatial region. The inherited actor remains the swimmer and traverser;
the region fills environment, not destination, recipient or actor. The swimming
constitutes the traversal during this same event. The traversal heads from a
contextual side toward an opposed side and does not assert arrival or completion.

The added relation is explicit conventional meaning; generic movement, swimming,
or the region's noun label alone does not entail it. This compound does not add
roots or alter the 54 prior compounds. Its actor slot is forwarded through two
reference levels; its region slot is local. Existing role and convention music
carry both bindings without allocating a new grammar operator.

Supported wording begins with progressive activity: “Eli is swimming across the
river.” Negation, questions and past-progressive time wrap the whole traversal.
Simple-present habits and completed “swam across” need separate aspect/completion
semantics and remain unfinished. Runtime 0/1/2 does not fill that gap.

Verification must recover region and swimmer separately from MIDI, preserve
reversals, reject missing bindings, and prove all previous definitions unchanged.
This reviewed specialization is not a claim of general path grammar, inferred
geographic identity, live listening validation or complete translator coverage.
