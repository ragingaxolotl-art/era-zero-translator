# MAT:200–222 Collision Report

Status: **peer-review evidence — pending delegated approval**

## Core ownership rule

The proposed block owns physical work capacity, directed material coupling, and systemic material interdependence. It does not own motion, form, topology, ownership, access, communication, data dependency, purpose, damage, repair, formal permission, quantity, probability, or universal law.

## High-risk English traps

- **Can** may mean physical capacity, permission, knowledge, probability, or opportunity. Only the physical-work sense is MAT:200–202.
- **Available** does not mean permitted, scheduled, accessible, or certain to be used.
- **Connection** may be structural, communicative, social, logical, or physical. MAT requires a physical-influence coupling.
- **Active coupling** requires current physical influence, not mere contact or adjacency.
- **System** and **network** do not imply material interdependence; topology is STR and information exchange is CON/DAT.
- **Conservation** as a law is ORI/LOG. A concrete material system may instantiate that law without making MAT:220 its universal statement.
- **Possible** at MAT:222 does not mean designed, planned, scheduled, probable, ready, permitted, or future-certain.
