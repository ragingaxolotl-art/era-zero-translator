The logical dependency is the approved **caused displacement in a physical frame** (`CMP:82dc9c681fbaf7944cb7b85b`, convention 71), already reused by lifting and lowering. Placement adds the same target's completed arrival (`MOV:110`) as the semantic head. No new root family is needed. Convention 85 explicitly binds the displaced bearer to the arriving mover and gives the outer sentence actor, target and destination roles. Mere adjacent roots would not establish these identities or causal connection.

Arrival is the endpoint of the caused movement. The actor need not arrive; a robot can put a box at a station while remaining elsewhere. Active physical influence is required during the causal phase, not after completion. Neither holding nor release is a necessary dependency. A placement can finish while the actor still holds the object. Being on a support or inside a container requires a further relation and must not be silently inferred from **at**.
