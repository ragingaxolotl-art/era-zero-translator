# CRE:200–222 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges all nine provisional Creation relation/capacity meanings. It compares five genuinely different architectures per address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and routes 115 English concepts without forcing broad words into atoms.

## Packet-level design decision

The strongest architecture makes `CRE:2xx` the substrate-neutral productive-affordance layer paired with canonical `CRE:1xx`. The inward triplet states one entity's capacity to form or modify a retained generative pattern, the outward triplet states a generative relation from one source toward one distinct prospective artifact, and the systemic triplet states multiple participants' capacity to generate one jointly constituted result in one shared production domain. Persistent, operative, and latent supply the exact third-trit contrast.

Persistent identity may co-occur with operative or latent status; operative and latent are mutually exclusive at one reference time. Lexical latency means an existing but nonoperative affordance, not uncertainty, hiddenness, undeveloped talent, disputed authorship, probability, scheduling, or future time. Runtime `[2]/num` remains uncertainty about the selected status. The design rejects the provisional mixture of mental agency, current production, authorship, ownership, causal influence, provenance, composability, structured possibility, and unrealized results. Creation entities remain `CRE:0xx`; actual productive processes remain `CRE:1xx`; agency, authorship, provenance, algorithms, media, structure, quality, and modality remain separate or compounded.

### D. Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** persistent internal pattern-forming capacity

**Invariant:** A persistent capacity within one reference entity to form or modify a generative pattern while retaining it within that entity, independently of whether that capacity is presently operative.

**Inclusion boundary:** Includes only the persistent internal pattern-forming capacity as a substrate-neutral productive-process affordance at inward scope. The capacity concerns productive formation or modification of a generative pattern retained within one entity and does not itself produce an outward artifact. The pattern, algorithm, medium, actual transformation, and result remain separate. It is neutral about Creation-entity identity, mental agency, physical medium, formal algorithm, structural mechanics, creator role, authorship, originality, quality, value, resource sufficiency, scheduling, probability, timing, and cause.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The generative pattern, generated artifact, or shared generative repertoire as an entity, and its persistent, manifest, or dormant status.
- The completed, active, or nonactualized pattern-formation, artifact-generation, or systemic co-generation process and any quality result.
- Mental imagination, general agency, specific encodings, formal algorithms, physical tools, media, structural arrangements, credentials, or computational resources.
- Physical, biological, communicative, cognitive, informational, logical, structural, restorative, or adverse capacity or transformation.
- Authorship, attribution, provenance, originality, ownership, authority, formal possibility, composability alone, quality, performance, or resource sufficiency.
- Observer knowledge, evidence, intention, plan, readiness, queue, schedule, future time, unrestricted possibility, necessity, probability, or cause.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_2`: Selects a productive-process capacity or qualifying generative relation rather than a Creation entity, actual productive process, creator, physical mechanism, result value, or unrestricted modality.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward/self scope: one reference entity and its capacity to form or modify a retained generative pattern and no other Creation-family column.
- `third_trit`: Selects the persistent status of the same productive affordance while leaving runtime truth status, actual production, quality, authorship, observer knowledge, tense, probability, permission, and scheduling separate.

**Nearest neighbors:** `CRE:201`, `CRE:202`, `CRE:210`, `CRE:220`, `CRE:000`, `CRE:100`

**Same-family boundary:** `CRE:0xx` owns generative patterns, generated artifacts, and shared generative repertoires as entities. `CRE:1xx` owns the corresponding completed, active, or nonactualized productive transformations. `CRE:2xx` owns only typed productive enablement: internal pattern-forming capacity, source-artifact generative relations, and systemic co-generative capacity. A manifest pattern does not entail capacity, an affordance does not entail an actual process, and a completed process does not entail continuing capacity, quality, or authorship. Persistent identity may co-occur with operative or latent status; operative and latent are mutually exclusive for the same affordance at one reference time. Lexical latency affirms an existing but nonoperative affordance; runtime `[2]/num` separately marks uncertainty about any selected status.

**Runtime-state interpretation:**

- `[0]`: Denies that this internal pattern-forming capacity persists in the stated production context; it does not select the operative or latent sibling and does not establish a different agency, authorship, or quality relation.
- `[1]`: Affirms that this internal pattern-forming capacity persists independently of current operation, without asserting that a productive process is occurring or that any result quality holds.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether this persistent internal pattern-forming capacity claim holds; runtime uncertainty does not change the lexical root into operative or latent status.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** Lexical third trit `0` permanently selects the persistent status of this productive affordance. Runtime `[2]/num` instead leaves the already-selected status claim unresolved; it never changes affordance status. Thus a latent root affirmed with `[1]` is a confirmed existing-but-nonoperative productive affordance, while a persistent or operative root carrying `[2]/num` remains lexically persistent or operative but uncertain as a token claim.
