# WRN:100–122 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges all nine provisional Warning process meanings from first principles. It compares five genuinely different semantic architectures at every address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and routes 93 English concepts without forcing broad words into atoms.

## Packet-level decision

The strongest architecture makes `WRN:1xx` a substrate-neutral adverse-transformation grid. `WRN:100–102` expresses adverse change originating within one reference entity. `WRN:110–112` expresses adverse change caused in one target by one distinct source. `WRN:120–122` expresses one correlated adverse condition becoming distributed across multiple participants in a shared system. The third trit changes only completion phase.

The grid composes directly with canonical `WRN:0xx`: internal degradation may yield or express an intrinsic fault, target impairment may realize the harmful potential of an external hazard, and systemic propagation may yield or extend a distributed fault. It excludes physical mechanics, generic change, diagnosis, evidence, warning communication, containment, repair, blame, probability, planning, and continuing vulnerability or exposure relations.

### Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** completed internal degradation

**Invariant:** Completion of an adverse transformation originating within one reference entity by which that entity enters a less acceptable condition.

**Boundary:** Includes only a completed substrate-neutral adverse transformation that originates within one reference entity and changes that entity toward a less acceptable condition without requiring a distinct external source. If a distinct external source causes the change, use target impairment; if one condition distributes across several participants, use systemic propagation. The transformation may be realized physically, biologically, computationally, informationally, socially, or conceptually, but it does not itself assert the bearer, concrete mechanics, movement path, evidence, observer knowledge, formal invalidity, blame, warning communication, response, repair, probability, schedule, or causal history beyond the scope roles required by the invariant.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The fault, hazard, distributed incident, bearer, source, target, participant, material object, organism, software component, institution, or data record as an entity or condition.
- Spatial movement, route traversal, physical force, collision, fracture, heating, biological mechanism, data mutation, destruction, or generation as such without the WRN adverse transformation claim.
- Planning, intention, staging, queueing, scheduling, permission, readiness, probability, futurity, obligation, or specification—especially for lexical 1x2.
- Detection, observation, diagnosis, classification, monitoring, evidence collection, belief revision, attribution, or measurement of an adverse condition.
- Warning, signaling, alerting, broadcasting, blocking, isolation, containment, shutdown, evacuation, coordination, repair, restoration, stabilization, or recovery.
- Susceptibility, vulnerability, exposure, exploitability, dependency, propagation pathway, probability, severity, blame, intention, or other continuing capacity or relation.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_1`: Selects an adverse process, action, or transformation rather than an adverse-condition entity, susceptibility or exposure relation, evidence record, warning message, or response.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward/self scope: this root originates within one reference entity and changes that entity toward a less acceptable condition without requiring a distinct external source; no competing family or temporal phase is added by this axis.
- `third_trit`: Selects the completed phase of the same internal-degradation process; planning, queueing, schedule, futurity, permission, probability, and runtime truth status remain separate.

**Nearest neighbors:** `WRN:101`, `WRN:102`, `WRN:110`, `WRN:120`, `WRN:000`, `WRN:200`

**Same-family boundary:** WRN:0xx owns adverse-condition entities: intrinsic faults, distinct external hazards, and correlated distributed faults. WRN:1xx owns adverse transformations toward less acceptable conditions. WRN:2xx remains reserved for susceptibility, exposure, and systemic propagation-enabling capacities or relations. Internal degradation has no required distinct external source. Target impairment preserves one source and one target as distinct roles. Systemic propagation requires one correlated adverse condition to become distributed across multiple participants. A completed WRN:1xx root entails only that the stated adverse transformation reached completion at the reference time; it does not entail persistent fault identity, present manifestation, evidence, detection, severity, containment, repair need, or a continuing WRN:2xx relation unless separately asserted.

**Runtime meanings:**

- `[0]`: Denies that this internal-degradation process reached completion in the stated warning context; it does not assert that the active or nonactualized sibling holds.
- `[1]`: Affirms that this internal-degradation process reached its completed phase, without asserting evidence, attribution, severity, permanent damage, ongoing manifestation, containment, repairability, or recovery.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether this internal-degradation process reached completion or whether that completed result remains applicable; the lexical root stays completed.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** The lexical third trit permanently selects the completed member of this process triplet. Runtime `[2]/num` instead qualifies the truth, applicability, or resolution of a token of that already-selected root; it never changes the root into a planned, future, or different-phase process.
