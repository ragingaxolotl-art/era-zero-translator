# Running toward a destination

Convention 24 specializes the existing running compound with MOV:111 approach.
It binds one actor occurrence into both the nested running activity and approach,
using an explicit same-argument constraint. The destination is a distinct role;
its sound label does not assert receipt, ownership or intentional pursuit.
The same-event relation is added conventional meaning, audible in its frame.
Running and a separate concurrent approach do not meet this definition.

The progressive toward construction asserts ongoing approach by running, without
arrival. In-place running alone cannot satisfy it. The prior running compound
still permits in-place execution. Runtime 0/1/2 remains independent of lexical
MOV:111 and of negation or past-time scope. All prior definitions are retained.

This is a dependency for later to-destination, completion and simple-present
aspect composition. Those constructions must not silently substitute ongoing
approach for arrival or a habitual event type. No new root family is required.

recovery of runner and destination, reversed participants producing different
meanings, missing or unequal repeated actor bindings rejected, and unchanged
previous compound definitions. Live listening and broad language coverage remain
unverified.
