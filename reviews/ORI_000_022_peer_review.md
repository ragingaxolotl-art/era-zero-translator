# ORI:000–022 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges all nine provisional Origin entity/condition meanings. It compares five genuinely different architectures per address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and routes 115 English concepts without forcing broad words into atoms.

## Packet-level design decision

The strongest architecture makes `ORI:0xx` the substrate-neutral ontological-condition layer. The inward triplet qualifies source-grounding, the outward triplet qualifies one distinct referent's existence in a relevant reality, and the systemic triplet qualifies a shared world as a whole. Persistent, occurrent/operative, and latent supply the exact third-trit contrast.

Persistent identity may co-occur with occurrence or latency; occurrent/operative and latent are mutually exclusive at one reference time. Lexical latency means membership without present expression, not uncertainty, hiddenness, possibility, counterfactuality, scheduling, or future time. Runtime `[2]/num` remains uncertainty about the selected claim. The design rejects the provisional mixture of causation, actuality, observability, emergence, propositional fact, natural-law content, temporal unfolding, and modal possibility. Processes remain `ORI:1xx`; causal, modal, temporal, and provenance relations remain `ORI:2xx` candidates for later review; matter, cognition, representation, formal truth, and production remain separate or compounded.

### D. Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** persistent source-ground condition

**Invariant:** A persistent ontological condition by which one referent is grounded as a source from which another state of affairs may originate, independently of whether that grounding is presently operative.

**Inclusion boundary:** Includes only the persistent source-ground condition as a substrate-neutral ontological qualification at inward scope. The condition qualifies source-grounding only. It does not assert a particular causal link, creator, temporal beginning, physical substrate, or produced result. It is neutral about substance, location, cognition, evidence, representation, logical validity, agency, authority, productive method, hazard, repair, scheduling, exact time, probability, and causal mechanism.

**Excluded meanings:**

- A causal link, temporal beginning, production event, authorship claim, or historical provenance relation.
- A material object, living agent, mental representation, data record, proposition, structural arrangement, warning, or repair condition.
- Observation, detectability, evidence, truth, validity, certainty, consensus, permission, or authority.
- Unrestricted possibility, necessity, probability, counterfactuality, schedule, future time, or mere nonexistence.
- The content of a natural law, algorithm, causal mechanism, physical mechanism, social convention, or formal model.
- Any claim that persistence entails current expression, or that latency entails uncertainty, concealment, planning, or futurity.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_0`: Selects an ontological entity or condition rather than an originating process, causal or modal relation, observation, representation, or physical constitution.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward/self scope: one referent qualified as a source-ground for an originating state of affairs and no other Origin-family column.
- `third_trit`: Selects the persistent status of the same ontological condition while leaving runtime assertion status, observer knowledge, clock time, probability, truth evaluation, causation, and physical realization separate.

**Nearest neighbors:** `ORI:001`, `ORI:002`, `ORI:010`, `ORI:020`, `ORI:000`, `ORI:100`

**Same-family boundary:** `ORI:0xx` owns ontological conditions: source-ground, distinct existence, and shared-world order. `ORI:1xx` is reserved for origin/reality processes such as beginning, actualization, or world change. `ORI:2xx` is reserved for causal, modal, temporal, or provenance relations and affordances. An ontological condition does not entail a cause, process, representation, observation, or physical substrate. Persistent identity may co-occur with occurrent/operative or latent status; occurrent/operative and latent are mutually exclusive for the same condition at one reference time. Lexical latency affirms membership without present expression; runtime `[2]/num` separately marks uncertainty about any selected claim.

**Runtime-state interpretation:**

- `[0]`: Denies that this source-ground condition persists in the stated reality; it does not select the occurrent or latent sibling and does not deny the referent itself.
- `[1]`: Affirms that this source-ground condition persists independently of present expression, without asserting a cause, observation, proof, or physical realization.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether this persistent source-ground condition claim holds; runtime uncertainty does not change the lexical root into operative or latent status.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** Lexical third trit `0` permanently selects the persistent status of this ontological condition. Runtime `[2]/num` instead leaves the already-selected claim unresolved; it never changes lexical status. Thus a latent root affirmed with `[1]` confirms membership without present expression, while a persistent or occurrent root carrying `[2]/num` remains lexically persistent or occurrent but uncertain as a token claim.
