Status: two reviewed conventional specializations under the user-directed

Both entries reference `CMP:ade73037114406a2cd91ce13` **version 1.0.0** as their

| Specialization | Compound | Convention code | Added commitment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Optical registration | CMP:bcfd189eb305cf364ada975a | 1 | The registered information is carried by light, including visible, infrared or ultraviolet light. |
| Acoustic registration | CMP:42e9bd596b35612ed6cbe3ce | 2 | The registered information is carried by a propagating mechanical pressure or elastic vibration signal through a material medium. |

These are physical registration channels. They do not assert conscious visual or
auditory experience, object identification, attention, or understanding. Optical
registration does not identify color, brightness or a human-visible wavelength.
Acoustic registration does not identify speech, pitch, loudness, or a human-audible
frequency. Static physical contact alone is not acoustic registration. No claim
is made that acoustic registration and tactile sensation are mutually exclusive:
physical carrier and conscious sensory modality are different distinctions.

## Why a single versioned reference is sufficient

The additional channel commitment supplies the semantic distinction. Adding a
redundant root just to make a two-node graph would falsely suggest that root
logically entails the channel. The conventional-compound extension therefore
permits a single **compound reference** plus explicit additional meaning. A lone
unmarked reference is an alias, not a new approved dictionary meaning. A lone root
cannot use this exception to introduce a disguised new atomic family.

Expansion opens the channel convention, opens the inherited registration
convention, performs the inherited roots, and closes both scopes in reverse
order. The lexical payload is unchanged. Only its external head receives runtime
0, 1, or 2; neither convention code is a runtime ending. Codes 0, 1 and 2 happen
to be the first registry identifiers, but each occupies the full eight-trit
lexical frame and remains separate from runtime state.

## Contrasts and ordinary phrases

“The sensor is registering optical input from the bell” and “The sensor is
registering acoustic input from the bell” keep identical source and receiver
references. The channel changes their meaning and music. The noun “bell” does
not choose a channel; an optical sensor may register light from the bell while
an acoustic sensor registers a vibration signal from it.

Changing only the runtime ending must not switch channels. Negation and modality
must preserve both the channel and inherited registration scope. The generic
registration sentence remains less specific, with only the foundation convention.

The implementation tests inspect version pins, expansion order, nested musical
frames, source/receiver roles, all three endings, and Ruby/browser parity. MIDI
frame reading recovers convention codes without source IR. This is not yet an
independent inverse of the complete sentence or a listener comprehension study.

Seeing and hearing remain pending. They need explicit experience/access
contracts; these registration channels must not be renamed into conscious
perception. Later compounds should reuse whichever of these foundations their
actual definitions entail.
