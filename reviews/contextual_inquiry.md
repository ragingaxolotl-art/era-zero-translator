# Contextual inquiry content before curiosity

The failed phrase “Orin is curious about the map” identifies a topic without
specifying whether the relevant questions concern its maker, accuracy, contents,
use or something else. Choosing one question in the translator would add meaning.
Treating the topic itself as a proposition would erase the question/content type.

EZCONTEXTUAL-INQUIRY-1 therefore contains the existing validated topic document
under a contextual question-set selector. Its relevant questions remain unresolved
until context identifies them. It supplies neither a particular question nor any
answer. This is content that a later cognitive predicate can take as an argument;
it asserts no knowledge, desire, curiosity, ignorance, attention or seeking action.
“Unresolved” describes the translator's question selection, not the participant's
knowledge. Multiple uses with the same topic are not evidence of identical external
question sets; discourse-level content identities remain unfinished.

Music reuses the topic/reference events within a draft inquiry wrapper at ±38.
Separate opt-in prevents old contextual-content decoders from treating an inquiry
as a bare topic. Twenty-one authored cases recover the topic and contract from
MIDI across seven reference forms and three anchors. Topic-only music rejects as
inquiry, inquiry rejects as topic-only content, and rehashed invented questions,
answer commitments and curiosity assertions reject. Frame collisions are checked
against existing canonical and draft grammar inventories. No root, compound,
runtime interpretation or existing sound is reassigned.

This content module alone does not translate “is curious about.” The subsequent
`reviews/curiosity.md` draft binds experiencer and inquiry through an explicit
epistemic-interest convention using the existing desire candidate, with sentence
composition, scoped/runtime music, Ruby regeneration, UI and a clean synthetic
WAV recovery. Broader grammar and physical recording evidence remain open. The new
standalone content profile is intentionally absent from full-message dispatch;
it must not be reported as a translated sentence. Independent Ruby validation and regeneration now match all 21 standalone content
performances, including seven reference forms and three anchors. A subsequent
curiosity sentence also exercises this content in blind synthetic WAV recovery;
standalone content WAV tests and physical recordings remain unfinished.
