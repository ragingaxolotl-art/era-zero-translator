# CON:200–222 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges every provisional meaning in the Connection capacity/relationship layer. It compares five genuinely different semantic architectures per address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and tests 73 English concepts without forcing all senses into atoms.

## Packet-level design decision

The strongest architecture completes one coherent three-layer family: `CON:0xx` names communicative loci, `CON:1xx` names exchange processes through those loci, and `CON:2xx` names communicative participation affordance or relations among participant endpoints. The second trit selects endpoint-internal capacity, directed target relation, or shared co-participation relation; the third trit changes only lexical condition.

This design rejects the Phase 2 mixture of receiving, responding, translation, dialogue, social bond, network topology, routing, and interoperability. Those concepts remain processes or typed compounds. An operative capacity or relation never entails traffic; a relation never entails reachability, permission, friendship, protocol compatibility, or channel existence.

### D. Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** enduring communicative participation capacity

**Invariant:** A persistent capacity of an endpoint to participate in communicative exchange.

**Inclusion boundary:** Includes only the enduring capacity by which an endpoint can participate, relates communicatively to a distinct target, or stands with multiple endpoints as co-participants in an exchange context. It is neutral about endpoints and channels as loci, actual message traffic, content, physical substrate, cognition, topology, protocol, reachability, social identity, and scheduling.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The source endpoint, target endpoint, shared channel, interface, device, address record, participant set, or topology as an entity.
- Expression, reception, distribution, dialogue, broadcast, negotiation, acknowledgement, or any other communicative event or traffic.
- A physical link, route, movement, carrier, transceiver, permission, authentication, protocol compatibility, logical reachability, failure, repair, or queue state.
- Friendship, kinship, affection, community identity, structural membership, attention, understanding, agreement, or joint mental state.
- Intention, plan, schedule, probability, future time, or guaranteed later realization—especially for lexical third-trit 2.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_2`: Selects a capacity or relationship. enduring communicative participation capacity is neither a communicative locus (`CON:0xx`) nor an expression, reception, or distribution process (`CON:1xx`).
- `second_trit`: Selects inward/self scope: endpoint-internal participation affordance, a directed relation to a distinct target, or a relation shared among multiple participant endpoints.
- `third_trit`: Selects the stable or enduring lexical member while leaving runtime assertion status, temporal scheduling, probability, permission, and failure to separate grammar or roots.

**Nearest neighbors:** `CON:201`, `CON:202`, `CON:210`, `CON:220`, `CON:000`, `CON:100`

**Same-family boundary:** `CON:0xx` owns communicative loci, `CON:1xx` owns exchange processes through those loci, and `CON:2xx` owns substrate-neutral communicative participation capacity or participant relations. An active capacity or relation never entails active traffic.

**Runtime-state interpretation:**

- `[0]`: Denies that the enduring condition named by enduring communicative participation capacity holds in the stated context; it does not assert the active or dormant/unresolved sibling.
- `[1]`: Affirms the persistent capacity or relation named by enduring communicative participation capacity, even if no exchange is occurring at this moment.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether the enduring condition named by enduring communicative participation capacity holds; it does not convert the lexeme into its active or dormant/unresolved sibling.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** Lexical third trit `0` permanently selects the stable or enduring member of this triplet. Runtime `[2]/num` instead leaves the truth or applicability of that already-selected lexeme unresolved; it never changes the lexical member.

### D. Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** operative shared-participation relation

**Invariant:** A communicative relation among multiple endpoints that presently operates to constitute them as co-participants in one shared exchange context.

**Inclusion boundary:** Includes only the presently operative shared relation by which an endpoint can participate, relates communicatively to a distinct target, or stands with multiple endpoints as co-participants in an exchange context. It is neutral about endpoints and channels as loci, actual message traffic, content, physical substrate, cognition, topology, protocol, reachability, social identity, and scheduling.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The source endpoint, target endpoint, shared channel, interface, device, address record, participant set, or topology as an entity.
- Expression, reception, distribution, dialogue, broadcast, negotiation, acknowledgement, or any other communicative event or traffic.
- A physical link, route, movement, carrier, transceiver, permission, authentication, protocol compatibility, logical reachability, failure, repair, or queue state.
- Friendship, kinship, affection, community identity, structural membership, attention, understanding, agreement, or joint mental state.
- Intention, plan, schedule, probability, future time, or guaranteed later realization—especially for lexical third-trit 2.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_2`: Selects a capacity or relationship. operative shared-participation relation is neither a communicative locus (`CON:0xx`) nor an expression, reception, or distribution process (`CON:1xx`).
- `second_trit`: Selects systemic/shared scope: endpoint-internal participation affordance, a directed relation to a distinct target, or a relation shared among multiple participant endpoints.
- `third_trit`: Selects the active or presently operative lexical member while leaving runtime assertion status, temporal scheduling, probability, permission, and failure to separate grammar or roots.

**Nearest neighbors:** `CON:220`, `CON:222`, `CON:201`, `CON:211`, `CON:021`, `CON:121`

**Same-family boundary:** `CON:0xx` owns communicative loci, `CON:1xx` owns exchange processes through those loci, and `CON:2xx` owns substrate-neutral communicative participation capacity or participant relations. An active capacity or relation never entails active traffic.

**Runtime-state interpretation:**

- `[0]`: Denies that the presently operative condition named by operative shared-participation relation holds now; persistence, latency, or future operation is not thereby decided.
- `[1]`: Affirms that operative shared-participation relation is presently available or operative, without asserting that expression, reception, or distribution traffic is occurring.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether operative shared-participation relation is presently operative; it does not select the enduring or dormant/unresolved lexical sibling.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** Lexical third trit `1` permanently selects the active or presently operative member of this triplet. Runtime `[2]/num` instead leaves the truth or applicability of that already-selected lexeme unresolved; it never changes the lexical member.
