# Reusable conjunction across cognitive drafts

EZCOGNITIVE-CONJUNCTION-1 binds two to eight complete declarative members under
all-of conjunction. Current members are curiosity, understanding, route learning
and comprehension acquisition. Each member remains a fully validated document;
its participants, contextual content, local time/negation and runtime trits are
retained. Acquisition results remain bound to culmination inside their member.
Conjunction adds no ordering, causal link, shared speaker-independent referent or
claim that the same person's separate cognitive contents are identical.

The English entry point splits unquoted “and” only when every segment is a
supported complete clause. Quoted name labels containing “and” stay intact.
Ambiguous member readings and personal pronouns require the existing explicit
choices; errors identify the clause needing them. A trailing runtime belongs to
its own clause. Omitted subjects, object coordination, question conjunctions,
disjunction and arbitrary canonical/draft mixtures are not
silently inferred. This is reusable composition within four implemented drafts,
not a claim of unrestricted English coordination.

Music reuses existing all-of/member frames and unchanged member performance
notes, with one final assertion contour. No frame, root, compound or runtime
meaning is added. The inverse uses audible frame boundaries to recover each
member through the shared semantic decoders, with recursive conjunction disabled
for this flat profile. It does not receive source documents, clause labels or
expected notes. Reconstructing a neutral member contour is a grammar operation:
declarative members are required by the validated outer all-of profile.

Forty-eight authored pair/runtime combinations recover meanings equal to each
independently decoded member. Tests cover local negation, differing event/result
trits, content-source time, explicit interpretation and singular-they binding,
quoted-name splitting and corrupt member rejection. One clean synthetic WAV
recovers a conjunction of negated curiosity and comprehension acquisition with
separate runtimes and past speech content through shared WAV-only dispatch. The
decoder receives no source English, IR or expected notes. This is not physical
recording evidence. The main interface now routes these conjunctions to a draft
card with separate clause reading choices and pronoun references. Actual handler
tests compose and export complete messages, recover both member meanings from
MIDI, preserve distinct runtimes, resolve acquisition-result readings and discard
stale source/binding outputs. Independent Ruby validation and regeneration now match 50 complete performances,
including the 48 pair/runtime combinations and three/eight-member transposed
messages. Ruby composes directly from validated member events, independently of
the JavaScript MIDI-segment construction. Rehashed operator/meaning changes,
question members and oversized groups reject. Existing standalone member MIDI
parity remains passing after exposing reusable Ruby event functions. Live
layout/listening, broader nested scope, broader member types, held-out coverage and
physical recording validation remain open.


## Explicit whole-message negation

“It is not true that A and B” now places one negation outside the all-of group.
Member negation remains inside its member, so not-both differs from neither.
The optional exact `scopes: [{operator: 'negation'}]` field is present only for
this extension; old unscoped document identities and performances are unchanged.
Existing negation frames surround the complete conjunction. The inverse identifies
member boundaries at the conjunction's actual depth and retains outer scope.

Nine contrast/runtime cases match independent Ruby regeneration. Independent
Boolean assignments distinguish not-both, neither and negation of a conjunction
with one negative member. These assignments test scope semantics and never use
runtime trits as truth. The interface explains the outer negation and exports the
complete scoped document. Repeated or unsupported outer-scope lists reject.
This does not add arbitrary nested scopes, quantifier negation, or pragmatic
interpretation of “not A and B” without explicit whole-message wording.

One authored clean synthetic WAV also recovers outer and inner negation together
with comprehension-acquisition and past speech content from WAV alone. This is
not physical recording or broad channel evidence.


## Possibility and ordered outer scope

Explicit “It is possible that A and B” preserves joint possibility over the
complete conjunction, without asserting either member as actual. “It is not
possible that” uses negation outside possibility; “It is possible that it is not
true that” places possibility outside negation. Existing possibility/negation
frames express this order. No probability, confidence percentage, permission,
actual outcome or new root is inferred. Optional scopes now admit exactly the
single negation, single possibility, and these two ordered pairs; old unscoped
and single-negation documents retain their identities and sounds.

Nine authored runtime/scope cases match independent Ruby and musical inverses.
An independent finite-world model demonstrates that joint possibility differs
from each member being separately possible, and that not-possible differs from
possible-not. Runtime trits do not enter this truth model. The UI explains the
chosen outer scope and exports the complete ordered document. Broader modal
kinds, arbitrary scope nesting and separate member possibility remain open.

One authored clean synthetic WAV also recovers possible-not scope around a
conjunction with inner negation, independent runtimes and past speech content,
without source English or hidden meaning supplied. Physical audio remains untested.
