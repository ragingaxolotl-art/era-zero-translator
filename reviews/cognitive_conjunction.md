# Reusable conjunction across cognitive drafts

EZCOGNITIVE-CONJUNCTION-1 binds two to eight complete declarative members under
all-of conjunction. Current members are curiosity, understanding, route learning
and comprehension acquisition. Each member remains a fully validated document;
its participants, contextual content, local time/negation and runtime trits are
retained. Acquisition results remain bound to culmination inside their member.
Conjunction adds no ordering, causal link, shared speaker-independent referent or
claim that the same person's separate cognitive contents are identical.

The English entry point splits unquoted “and” only when every segment is a
supported complete clause. Quoted name labels containing “and” stay intact.
Ambiguous member readings and personal pronouns require the existing explicit
choices; errors identify the clause needing them. A trailing runtime belongs to
its own clause. Omitted subjects, object coordination, question conjunctions,
disjunction, outer negation and arbitrary canonical/draft mixtures are not
silently inferred. This is reusable composition within four implemented drafts,
not a claim of unrestricted English coordination.

Music reuses existing all-of/member frames and unchanged member performance
notes, with one final assertion contour. No frame, root, compound or runtime
meaning is added. The inverse uses audible frame boundaries to recover each
member through the shared semantic decoders, with recursive conjunction disabled
for this flat profile. It does not receive source documents, clause labels or
expected notes. Reconstructing a neutral member contour is a grammar operation:
declarative members are required by the validated outer all-of profile.

Forty-eight authored pair/runtime combinations recover meanings equal to each
independently decoded member. Tests cover local negation, differing event/result
trits, content-source time, explicit interpretation and singular-they binding,
quoted-name splitting and corrupt member rejection. One clean synthetic WAV
recovers a conjunction of negated curiosity and comprehension acquisition with
separate runtimes and past speech content through shared WAV-only dispatch. The
decoder receives no source English, IR or expected notes. This is not physical
recording evidence. The main interface now routes these conjunctions to a draft
card with separate clause reading choices and pronoun references. Actual handler
tests compose and export complete messages, recover both member meanings from
MIDI, preserve distinct runtimes, resolve acquisition-result readings and discard
stale source/binding outputs. Independent Ruby conjunction regeneration, live
layout/listening, nested/outer scope, broader member types, held-out coverage and
physical recording validation remain open.
