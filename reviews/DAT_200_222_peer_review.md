# DAT:200–222 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges all nine provisional Data relation/capacity meanings. It compares five genuinely different architectures per address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and routes 125 English concepts without forcing broad words into atoms.

## Packet-level design decision

The strongest architecture makes `DAT:2xx` the substrate-neutral information-process-affordance layer paired with canonical `DAT:1xx`. The inward triplet states represented information's capacity for bounded transformation within one entity, the outward triplet states one source context's transferability relation to one distinct target, and the systemic triplet states capacity for coordinated information-state alignment across multiple participants under one synchronization criterion. Persistent, operative, and latent supply the exact third-trit contrast.

Persistent identity may co-occur with operative or latent status; operative and latent are mutually exclusive at one reference time. Lexical latency means an existing but nonoperative affordance, not uncertainty, hiddenness, brokenness, probability, permission, scheduling, or future time. Runtime `[2]/num` remains uncertainty about the selected status. The design rejects the provisional mixture of access, permission, retrieval, reference identity, dereference processes, broken links, integrity, provenance, consistency, replication, reconstruction, and possibility. Information entities remain `DAT:0xx`; actual information processes remain `DAT:1xx`; access, integrity, provenance, algorithms, media, authority, results, and modality remain separate or compounded.

#### stable access capacity

**Invariant candidate:** An entity has dependable permission and means to read, retain, or use its own information.

- Fit: The provisional titles use familiar data-relation vocabulary—access, retrieval, reference, dereference, integrity, provenance, consistency, and recoverability—and roughly reflect inward scope and persistent status.

#### persistent internal data transformability

**Invariant candidate:** A persistent capacity of represented information within one reference entity to undergo bounded internal data transformation, independently of whether that capacity is presently operative.

- Fit: First-trit 2 becomes the capacity or qualifying relation enabling the matching DAT:1xx process; the second trit changes only inward, target, or systemic scope; and the third changes only persistent, operative, or latent status.

#### persistent internal retrievability

**Invariant candidate:** A persistent information access or retrieval relation at inward scope.

#### persistent internal reference resolution

#### persistent internal integrity relation

### D. Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** persistent internal data transformability

**Invariant:** A persistent capacity of represented information within one reference entity to undergo bounded internal data transformation, independently of whether that capacity is presently operative.

**Inclusion boundary:** Includes only the persistent internal data transformability as a substrate-neutral information-process affordance at inward scope. The capacity concerns represented information within one entity without requiring a distinct external target. The datum, algorithm, medium, actual transformation, and result remain separate. It is neutral about information-object identity, storage medium, access authority, fidelity, integrity, provenance, bandwidth, resource sufficiency, observer belief, scheduling, probability, timing, and cause.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The internal datum, target reference, or shared dataset as an entity, and its persistent, manifest, or dormant status.
- The completed, active, or nonactualized transformation, target transfer, or synchronization process and any quality or acknowledgment result.
- Specific encodings, schemas, algorithms, storage devices, physical carriers, channels, credentials, bandwidth, or computational resources.
- Physical, biological, communicative, cognitive, logical, structural, restorative, creative, or adverse capacity or transformation.
- Access permission, retrieval, reference resolution, integrity, provenance, consistency, compatibility, ownership, authority, performance, or resource sufficiency.
- Observer knowledge, evidence, intention, plan, readiness, queue, schedule, future time, unrestricted possibility, necessity, probability, or cause.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_2`: Selects an information-process capacity or qualifying transfer relation rather than an information entity, actual data process, physical carrier, result value, or unrestricted modality.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward/self scope: represented information within one entity and its capacity for bounded internal transformation and no other Data-family column.
- `third_trit`: Selects the persistent status of the same information-process affordance while leaving runtime truth status, actual processing, quality, observer knowledge, tense, probability, permission, and scheduling separate.

**Nearest neighbors:** `DAT:201`, `DAT:202`, `DAT:210`, `DAT:220`, `DAT:000`, `DAT:100`

**Same-family boundary:** `DAT:0xx` owns information entities and their persistent, manifest, or dormant status. `DAT:1xx` owns the corresponding completed, active, or nonactualized information processes. `DAT:2xx` owns only typed process enablement: internal transformability, source-target transferability, and systemic synchronizability. A datum does not entail transformability, an affordance does not entail an actual process, and a completed process does not entail fidelity, persistence, or acknowledgment. Persistent identity may co-occur with operative or latent status; operative and latent are mutually exclusive for the same affordance at one reference time. Lexical latency affirms an existing but nonoperative affordance; runtime `[2]/num` separately marks uncertainty about any selected status.

**Runtime-state interpretation:**

- `[0]`: Denies that this internal data transformability persists in the stated information context; it does not select the operative or latent sibling and does not establish a different access or quality relation.
- `[1]`: Affirms that this internal data transformability persists independently of current operation, without asserting that a data process is occurring or that any result quality holds.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether this persistent internal data transformability claim holds; runtime uncertainty does not change the lexical root into operative or latent status.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** Lexical third trit `0` permanently selects the persistent status of this information-process affordance. Runtime `[2]/num` instead leaves the already-selected status claim unresolved; it never changes affordance status. Thus a latent root affirmed with `[1]` is a confirmed existing-but-nonoperative affordance, while a persistent or operative root carrying `[2]/num` remains lexically persistent or operative but uncertain as a token claim.
