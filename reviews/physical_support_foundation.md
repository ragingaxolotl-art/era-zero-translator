# Physical load support before carrying

Status: reviewed conventional foundation under the user-directed completion goal.
This is a new compound sense, not a new root or an altered atomic definition.

## Invariant and participants

A material source is presently transmitting physical influence to a distinct
material load such that the source bears at least part of the load and resists
the load's displacement under an applied force. Support can be direct contact or
a physical field. The force need not be gravity, and the load need not be at rest.
This is load-bearing support, not merely any influence that changes a trajectory.
It does not entail intention, consciousness, movement, complete immobilization,
or that the source bears the whole load without help.

The source slot denotes the supporting bearer; the target slot denotes the load.
MAT:010 qualifies the target slot, so the material-object restriction applies to
the actual named load. MAT:211 is the head: physical influence is transmitted by
this same source to this same target. Both slots require explicit entity bindings.
The load is distinct from the supporting source. Noun labels identify participants;
they do not infer an animate agent or manufacture a material meaning by spelling.

## Existing ingredients and the missing distinction

The reviewed 324-root inventory and 35-compound baseline supply physical influence,
material bodies, movement, structural engagement and ontological dependence. The
following closest candidates do not by themselves establish load-bearing support:

| Candidate | Counterexample |
|---|---|
| MAT:211 active material coupling | A lamp warms a box without bearing its load. |
| MOV:121 ongoing movement | A box falls unsupported. |
| MOV:211 live target-relative position | Two independently moving objects maintain a relative position without supporting each other. |
| STR:011 engaged structural interface | An engaged electrical connector can transmit a signal without bearing the connected object's load. |
| ORI dependence | A process may depend on data without physically supporting a body. |
| LIF:211 care | Caring for someone need not involve physical load support. |

The existing ingredients remain true of the chosen compound, but the load-bearing
commitment is additional. EZLEX-1 convention code 3 states and audibly distinguishes
it. This is not a claim that an unmarked MAT graph logically entails support, nor
an impossibility proof over every possible compound. No new 27-root family is needed
for this implementation. Existing root meanings and musical paths remain intact.

## Dependency order

1. Establish physical load support with source and target bindings.
2. Define carrying by reusing that exact support meaning, adding transport and the
   required participant identity and temporal relationships. Mere simultaneous
   support and an unrelated movement must not qualify.
3. Define lifting, lowering, putting down, and carrying to a recipient/destination
   only after direction, transition and endpoint semantics are explicit.

Pushing is not carrying merely because it moves a box: the floor may bear the
load while the pusher supplies a lateral force. A shelf supports a box without
carrying it anywhere. A moving carrier may support a load while both move; this
foundation does not decide the additional carrying claim. Grasping or holding an
object is also broader than the load-bearing relation in this entry.

## English scope and tests

The initial unambiguous construction is “X is physically supporting Y.” Bare
“supports,” “holds,” “carries,” and metaphorical support remain outside this entry.
The visible interpretation states the load-bearing sense. Negation and modality
scope over the relation; 0, 1 and 2 remain occurrence endings, not force levels,
load magnitudes, or new meanings of the supporting root.

Required contrasts include source/target reversal, support versus physical input
registration, a stationary shelf versus a moving carrier, and independent runtime
endings. Verify exact participant placement, the target's material qualifier,
reference/browser MIDI parity and recovery of the convention and role frames from
MIDI without source IR. Structural tests do not establish broad English coverage
or prove that carrying has already been implemented.
