# Mass properties inside noun phrases

The carrying milestone needs a property on the load, not an extra property on
the carrying action. This change adds `property_restriction` to a definite nominal
reference and keeps the property inside its audible reference boundary.
It introduces no root, family, or dictionary compound.

“Pip is carrying the heavy box toward Ada” now asks for a mass threshold before
composition. With a selected 20.5 kg standard, it refers to the box restricted to
mass greater than 20.5 kg. “Light” selects less than that threshold. The value is
an explicit contextual interpretation, not a universal English cutoff, weight
force, effort, or a new meaning of a root. Noun phonemes remain those of “box”.

The restriction follows that reference through both nested carrying slots. It
does not qualify Pip, Ada or the carrying predicate. Moving it to “the heavy robot”
changes its participant attachment. Action negation does not negate the box's
descriptive restriction. Action runtime 0/1/2 does not activate or deactivate the
restriction; those states still belong to their lexical occurrence.

The new ±21 grammar frame encloses the existing scalar comparison and exact
measurement, a head separator, and the nominal sound label. All previous root
melodies are unchanged. A bounded inverse reads MIDI alone and recovers the
dimension, threshold, relation, noun phonemes and enclosing audible roles. It
does not claim full sentence decoding, original spelling or discourse identity.

The test suite compares browser and Ruby formal outputs and MIDI across 36
adjective, participant, negation, question, command, past and runtime combinations.
It also checks equivalent units, changed thresholds, invalid property data and
quoted-name preservation. Existing whole-predicate standards remain supported.

Still open: “Pip carries the heavy box to Ada” requires simple-present/aspect
handling and the meaning of “to”, beyond the implemented progressive/toward
construction. Two independently restricted participants, adjective stacks,
quantified descriptions, general relative clauses and future-plus-standard
selection are not covered. No gate is closed by this bounded implementation.
