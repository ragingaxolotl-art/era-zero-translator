# CRE:000–022 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges all nine provisional Creation entity meanings. It compares five genuinely different architectures per address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and routes 125 English concepts without forcing broad words into atoms.

## Packet-level design decision

The strongest architecture makes `CRE:0xx` the substrate-neutral entity layer for generative sources and products. The inward triplet identifies one bounded form-specifying pattern maintained within a reference entity, the outward triplet identifies one bounded product of productive transformation, and the systemic triplet identifies one coordinated shared generative repertoire across multiple participants. Persistence, manifestation, and dormancy supply the exact third-trit contrast.

Each `...0` member asserts persistence of Creation-entity identity and is neutral about present manifestation, so it may co-occur with either its `...1` or `...2` sibling. The manifest `...1` and dormant `...2` siblings are mutually exclusive for the same entity at the same reference time. Runtime `[2]/num` remains uncertainty about the selected claim. The design rejects the provisional mixture of inspiration, process, testing, revision, structural systems, use, availability, and future realization. Actual productive transformations remain for `CRE:1xx`; generative capacities and relations remain for `CRE:2xx`; mental content, represented data, formal rules, structure, matter, communication, and time remain other families.

Thus a dormant root affirmed with `[1]` is a confirmed maintained-but-unmanifest generative entity, while a persistent or manifest root carrying `[2]/num` remains lexically persistent or manifest but uncertain as a token claim.
