# Completed running journeys

Convention 25 depends on exact destination-running convention 24 and MOV:110
arrival. The same actor and destination are required across approach and arrival.
The arrival is the culmination of the running event, not an unrelated later
arrival. Nested running and approach are internal phases, not current assertions.
Running partway and then traveling by a separate manner does not suffice.

This extends the existing running dependency chain without adding roots or
changing prior meanings. Runtime 0/1/2 is independent of completion and past tense.
Negating completed running denies that event; it does not assert a partial run.
Questions do not assert arrival. No continued presence, receipt or ownership is
implied by reaching a destination.

Initial English forms are ran to and did not run to, plus did questions and their
contractions. Simple-present runs to still requires an aspect choice. Verify
complete actor/destination identity, runtime, scopes, Ruby/browser parity and
independent MIDI recovery. Reject malformed approach/arrival root substitutions
and missing or inconsistent repeated arguments. These checks do not establish
live listening or complete English translation.

## Simple-present viewpoint follow-up

Runs to now requires a viewpoint choice. An unfolding reading uses the approach
compound; a bounded narrated journey uses completed running; a habit scopes the
completed event type without asserting a particular current arrival. Runs without
a destination and runs toward keep their activity/approach meaning under all three
viewpoints. Explicit habitually forms compose directly. Per-clause choices preserve
these distinctions in mixed sequences, conjunctions and conditionals. This supersedes
the earlier simple-present limitation; nested temporal modality remains unresolved.
