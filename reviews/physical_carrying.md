# Physical carrying semantic review

This is an author-reviewed convention under the translator-completion goal, not independent semantic validation. Reuse physical support 1.0.0 before defining carrying. Existing roots and previous compound definitions remain unchanged.

The supported material load is exactly the moving actor. Its carrier is distinct. Support and movement occur during the same interval, and the moving load-bearing engagement provides conveyance relative to the relevant frame. This covers a hand, tray, vehicle or conveyor belt without requiring intention or translation of the entire carrier. It excludes stationary support, unrelated simultaneous movement, pushing across an independently supporting surface, and flight after release. It does not introduce a destination or recipient.

Equal-slot and distinct-participant constraints are part of the content-addressed definition. Copying the same noun label is not evidence of identity.
