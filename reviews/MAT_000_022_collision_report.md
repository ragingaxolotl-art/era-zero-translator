# MAT:000–022 Collision Report

Status: **peer-review evidence — not canonical**

## Core ownership rule

The proposed block owns physical substrate, object, and aggregate entities/conditions at stable, active, or dormant lexical phases. It does not own identity, movement, cognition, access, structure, failure, repair, formal validity, data, creation, or ontology.

## Intra-Matter layer boundary

| Entity block | Reserved for MAT:100–122 review | Reserved for MAT:200–222 review |
|---|---|---|
| substrate/object/aggregate conditions | transformation, assembly, consumption, force/energy transfer, systemic exchange | integrity capacity, available work, stored potential, coupling, physical fit, conservation relationships |

## Canonical LIF/MOV audit

- LIF roots classify living identity, consciousness, life processes, vitality, and biosystems; Matter roots only supply physical bearers.
- MOV roots classify positions, routes, displacement, departure, arrival, and shared-frame movement; movement alone never selects an active Matter entity.
- A living, conscious, moving, material being therefore uses a graph with independent LIF, MOV, and MAT assertions rather than one overloaded atom.

## High-risk English traps

- **Body** is not automatically life or consciousness: combine MAT substrate with LIF when those meanings are asserted.
- **Active** does not mean moving, executing code, thinking, alive, or socially engaged; this block requires physical energetic expression.
- **Object** does not imply tool, resource, possession, target, or artifact.
- **Dormant** does not imply broken, inaccessible, stored, reserved, dead, or certain to reactivate.
- **Aggregate** does not imply structural organization, network topology, ownership, quantity, or resource purpose.
- **Flow** is not an entity meaning here. Use MOV for displacement and later MAT process roots for material exchange.
