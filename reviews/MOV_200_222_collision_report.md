# MOV:200–222 Collision Report

Status: **peer-review evidence — not canonical**

## Core boundary

The proposed 2xx layer owns capacity and positional relationships only. It does not own a mover, target, route, movement event, coordinate record, structural topology, permission, causal force, participant coordination, generated path, or universal modality.

## High-risk English traps

- **Can move** is `MOV:201`; **is moving** is an approved 1xx process.
- **Reachable** is a LOG conclusion over target relations, routes, and constraints—not `MOV:210` or `MOV:212` alone.
- **Tracking** combines `MOV:211` with DAT and often MIN; the Motion root does not own observation.
- **Formation coordination** combines `MOV:221` with CON; the positional configuration does not imply coordination.
- **Navigability** combines Motion route/configuration facts with STR topology and LOG constraints.
- **Possible path** is not `MOV:222`; that root concerns possible configurations, while routes remain `MOV:020–022` plus modality.
- Spatial metaphors such as “state reachable” use MOV only after the model explicitly declares a positional state space.

## Disposition

No blocking collision remains after the recommended narrowing. The candidates are separable from all 18 canonical Motion roots and from MAT, MIN, CON, STR, LOG, DAT, CRE, and ORI. They remain provisional until explicit user approval.
