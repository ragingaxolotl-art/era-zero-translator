# Ordinary universal and zero subjects

This grammar extension adds every/each plus a singular common-noun class, no plus a singular or plural class, and not every plus a singular class. It reuses nominal_class references, the existing group flag and the published all/none and outside-negation frames. It introduces no roots, dictionary senses, compounds or musical frames. Every and each have a distributive reading over members, not a new named individual or an exact cardinality.

Both earlier parsing passes run before the new quantified-subject pass, including the dated-sentence parser. Earlier all-the/none-of/a-few/nobody performances, dated states and names such as Every in dated actions stay exact. The extension uses the existing noun and predicate parsers, keeping the noun's singular/plural agreement separately from its class reference. Existing predicates, object descriptions, maintained-state conditions, physical/information sense choices and runtime 0/1/2 remain reusable. Noun labels remain phonemic references, not newly invented lexical roots or gender assignments.

No means that zero class members satisfy the entire scoped predicate. Not every denies that all do; it is compatible with none or some doing so, but differs on mixed and empty domains. Neither all nor none asserts that a member exists. Not every requires a counterexample under the existing classical quantifier convention. Test empty, all-false, mixed and all-true member sets rather than treating different score hashes as proof of these distinctions.

Every/each with a direct negative can mean that each member satisfies the negative or that the positive is not true for every member. A focused choice preserves either all outside the original negative predicate, or outside-negated all with exactly that direct negative removed. Negation may be selected this way only when it is the first scope after tense; it must not move across a nested possibility/ability. An explicit not every or no retains its written scope. The rendered interpretation explains the complete scope; it must not append an assertion that every member acted to a question, future, negative or modal reading.

Initially, the new subjects require one clause in one message. Bound individuals must not silently escape into a later inherited action or pronoun. Restricted subjects with adjective descriptions, object/cardinal quantification, new quantified date attachment and passive quantified patients remain unsupported. Existing accepted constructions remain preserved, rather than being redefined by this increment. Tense and modal scopes remain inside the existing quantified event frame; no wider temporal-domain or collective-action reading is inferred.

Validation must independently recover class identity, participant roles, quantifier, negative scope, tense/modal order, speech act and runtime from melody and expanded chords. Pin old performances and verify actual browser choices, downloaded songs, layout, keyboard controls and stale-result clearing. These checks do not establish unrestricted English or speaker-to-microphone recovery. This author review implements the user's standing grammar and usability goal; it does not assert separate user approval of each phrase.
