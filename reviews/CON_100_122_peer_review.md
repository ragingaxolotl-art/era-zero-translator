# CON:100–122 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges every Phase 2 meaning in the Connection process layer. It compares five genuinely different semantic architectures per address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and tests 62 English concepts without forcing all senses into atoms.

## Packet-level design decision

The strongest permanent architecture treats `CON:1xx` as **participant-addressed communicative exchange processes**: source expression, target reception, and shared distribution. Each triplet differs only by completed, active, or nonactualized phase. This is substrate-neutral and composes with canonical `CON:0xx` loci.

The center is availability in exchange, not information creation, physical propagation, comprehension, channel establishment, protocol, negotiation, or relationship formation. `DAT` owns the expression as content; `MIN` owns interpretation; `MAT/MOV` own carriers and displacement; `STR/LOG` own topology and rules; `CON:2xx` remains reserved for capacities and relations.

#### self-synchronization complete

**Invariant candidate:** An entity's internal participants or interfaces have completed a connection or synchronization process.

- Fit: The Phase 2 choice follows the inward/source and completed slot labels and selects salient Connection vocabulary such as synchronization, transmission, delivery, or broadcast.

- Conflicts and limitations: The nine current meanings do not share one process center, completed/active/nonactualized siblings are not exact phase variants, and several definitions encode intention, preparation, queueing, or network structure forbidden by the 1x2 clarification.

#### completed source expression

**Invariant candidate:** Completion of an act by which a source endpoint makes a communicative expression available to an exchange context.

- Fit: Every root is one communicative availability process; source, target, and shared scope follow the second trit exactly, while the third trit changes only realization phase.

- Conflicts and limitations: It deliberately leaves composition to CRE/DAT, comprehension to MIN, physical propagation to MAT/MOV, channel lifecycle to STR/CON:0xx, protocol to LOG, and reciprocal interaction to compounds.

#### completed self-synchronization

**Invariant candidate:** A completed process that aligns communicative state or timing at source-side scope.

- Fit: Synchronization and coordination are systemic Connection processes and have strong programming and distributed-system value.

#### completed conversational initiation

**Invariant candidate:** A completed discourse act performed at source-side scope in a participant exchange.

- Conflicts and limitations: It is anthropocentric, listening often requires MIN attention, acknowledgement requires LOG/DAT relation to a prior message, and the three triplets cannot remain exact phase variants without forcing unrelated discourse acts together.

### D. Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** completed source expression

**Invariant:** Completion of an act by which a source endpoint makes a communicative expression available to an exchange context.

**Inclusion boundary:** Includes only the completed participant-addressed act at inward/source scope by which a source endpoint makes an expression available. It is neutral about the expression's content, physical carrier, protocol, success quality, comprehension, response, and later use.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The endpoint, participant, shared channel, device, medium, route, or topology as an entity or condition.
- The message, signal, request, symbol, payload, record, draft, queue item, or protocol as content or structure.
- Physical propagation, movement, transduction, storage, computation, attention, interpretation, understanding, agreement, or belief change.
- Opening, closing, joining, repairing, authenticating, authorizing, scheduling, or configuring a channel or endpoint.
- Intention, readiness, permission, plan, schedule, queue status, probability, certainty, futurity, or obligation—especially for lexical 1x2 roots.
- Reciprocal conversation, acknowledgement, negotiation, consensus, request-response, or transport, which require compounds and explicit participant/content relations.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_1`: Selects a process, action, or transformation: completed source expression is an exchange act, not an endpoint/channel entity and not a bond, capacity, or compatibility relation.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward/source scope: it locates the exchange process at the originating endpoint and its act of making an expression available without changing the content or physical carrier.
- `third_trit`: Selects the completed process phase: the same exchange act is completed, while intention, planning, scheduling, queueing, probability, and future time remain outside the lexeme.

**Nearest neighbors:** `CON:101`, `CON:102`, `CON:110`, `CON:120`, `CON:000`, `CON:200`, `DAT:100`, `MIN:100`

**Same-family boundary:** CON:0xx owns the endpoint/channel loci through which exchange occurs; CON:1xx owns the expression, reception, and distribution processes themselves; CON:2xx remains reserved for capacities, bonds, compatibility, reachability, and possible exchange relations.

**Runtime-state interpretation:**

- `[0]`: States that completion of this source-expression act is false, unavailable as a valid result, or not established in the exchange context; it does not assert the active or nonactualized sibling.
- `[1]`: Affirms that this source-expression act reached its completed phase in the stated exchange context; the result does not entail reception, comprehension, response, or endpoint-wide uptake unless separately asserted.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether this source-expression act reached completion or whether that completion remains a valid result; it does not change the lexical process into an active or nonactualized sibling.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** The third lexical trit permanently selects the completed member of the source process triplet. Runtime `[2]/num` instead qualifies the assertion about that already-selected lexeme. Therefore CON:100[1] affirms a completed exchange act, while CON:100[2]/num leaves the assertion about that same completed act unresolved.

### D. Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** active shared distribution

**Invariant:** An ongoing act by which a communicative expression is made available across a shared channel to multiple participant endpoints.

**Inclusion boundary:** Includes only the ongoing participant-addressed act at shared/systemic scope by which an expression is made available across a shared channel to multiple endpoints. It is neutral about the expression's content, physical carrier, protocol, success quality, comprehension, response, and later use.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The endpoint, participant, shared channel, device, medium, route, or topology as an entity or condition.
- The message, signal, request, symbol, payload, record, draft, queue item, or protocol as content or structure.
- Physical propagation, movement, transduction, storage, computation, attention, interpretation, understanding, agreement, or belief change.
- Opening, closing, joining, repairing, authenticating, authorizing, scheduling, or configuring a channel or endpoint.
- Intention, readiness, permission, plan, schedule, queue status, probability, certainty, futurity, or obligation—especially for lexical 1x2 roots.
- Reciprocal conversation, acknowledgement, negotiation, consensus, request-response, or transport, which require compounds and explicit participant/content relations.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_1`: Selects a process, action, or transformation: active shared distribution is an exchange act, not an endpoint/channel entity and not a bond, capacity, or compatibility relation.
- `second_trit`: Selects shared/systemic scope: it locates the exchange process at the shared channel and availability of an expression to multiple endpoints without changing the content or physical carrier.
- `third_trit`: Selects the active process phase: the same exchange act is ongoing, while intention, planning, scheduling, queueing, probability, and future time remain outside the lexeme.

**Nearest neighbors:** `CON:120`, `CON:122`, `CON:101`, `CON:111`, `CON:021`, `CON:221`, `DAT:121`, `MIN:121`

**Same-family boundary:** CON:0xx owns the endpoint/channel loci through which exchange occurs; CON:1xx owns the expression, reception, and distribution processes themselves; CON:2xx remains reserved for capacities, bonds, compatibility, reachability, and possible exchange relations.

**Runtime-state interpretation:**

- `[0]`: States that this shared-distribution act is not presently ongoing or has ceased; it does not assert that the process completed or remains available as a nonactualized act.
- `[1]`: Affirms that this shared-distribution act is presently ongoing in the stated exchange context.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether this shared-distribution act is presently ongoing; it does not select either the completed or nonactualized lexical sibling.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** The third lexical trit permanently selects the active member of the shared process triplet. Runtime `[2]/num` instead qualifies the assertion about that already-selected lexeme.
