# LOG:200–222 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges all nine provisional Logic relation/capacity meanings. It compares five genuinely different architectures per address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and routes 123 English concepts without forcing broad words into atoms.

## Packet-level design decision

The strongest architecture makes `LOG:2xx` the substrate-neutral formal-evaluation-affordance layer paired with canonical `LOG:1xx`. The inward triplet states one entity's capacity for evaluation under its internal criterion, the outward triplet states one specified constraint's applicability to one distinct target, and the systemic triplet states capacity for one coordinated joint evaluation across multiple participants under one shared regime. Persistent, operative, and latent supply the exact third-trit contrast.

Persistent identity may co-occur with operative or latent status; operative and latent are mutually exclusive at one reference time. Lexical latency means an existing but nonoperative affordance, not uncertainty, missing evidence, probability, permission, scheduling, or future time. Runtime `[2]/num` remains uncertainty about the selected status. The design rejects the provisional mixture of decidability, computability, implication, entailment, active derivation, consistency, recursion, resource conditions, and formal possibility. Rule entities remain `LOG:0xx`; actual evaluations remain `LOG:1xx`; consequence, consistency, algorithms, authority, results, and modality remain separate or compounded.

### D. Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** persistent internal formal evaluability

**Invariant:** A persistent capacity of one reference entity to undergo formal evaluation under its applicable internal criterion, independently of whether that capacity is presently operative.

**Inclusion boundary:** Includes only the persistent internal formal evaluability as a substrate-neutral formal-evaluation affordance at inward scope. The capacity belongs to one entity without requiring a distinct external target. The criterion, evaluator, actual evaluation, data, and result remain separate. It is neutral about rule storage or authority, acceptance versus rejection, truth, proof correctness, resource sufficiency, computational complexity, observer belief, scheduling, probability, timing, and cause.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The formal criterion, target constraint, or shared regime as an entity, and its persistent, operative, or dormant rule status.
- The completed, active, or nonactualized evaluation process and any acceptance, rejection, truth, validity, satisfaction, or violation result.
- Data inputs, variables, bindings, records, schemas, source code, proof objects, algorithms, evaluators, or stored outputs.
- Physical, biological, communicative, structural, restorative, creative, or adverse capacity or transformation.
- Entailment, implication, consequence, consistency, compatibility, permission, authority, enforcement, sanction, performance, complexity, or resource sufficiency.
- Observer knowledge, evidence, intention, plan, readiness, queue, schedule, future time, unrestricted possibility, necessity, probability, or cause.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_2`: Selects a formal-evaluation capacity or applicability relation rather than a rule entity, an actual evaluation process, represented data, a result value, or unrestricted modality.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward/self scope: one reference entity and its capacity for evaluation under an applicable internal criterion and no other Logic-family column.
- `third_trit`: Selects the persistent status of the same formal-evaluation affordance while leaving runtime truth status, actual evaluation, result polarity, observer knowledge, tense, probability, permission, and scheduling separate.

**Nearest neighbors:** `LOG:201`, `LOG:202`, `LOG:210`, `LOG:220`, `LOG:000`, `LOG:100`

**Same-family boundary:** `LOG:0xx` owns formal constraint entities and their identity or operation. `LOG:1xx` owns the corresponding completed, active, or nonactualized evaluation processes. `LOG:2xx` owns only typed formal-evaluation enablement: internal evaluability, constraint-target applicability, and systemic joint-evaluability. A rule does not entail evaluability, evaluability does not entail an actual evaluation, and an evaluation does not entail a positive result. Persistent identity may co-occur with operative or latent status; operative and latent are mutually exclusive for the same affordance at one reference time. Lexical latency affirms an existing but nonoperative affordance; runtime `[2]/num` separately marks uncertainty about any selected status.

**Runtime-state interpretation:**

- `[0]`: Denies that this internal formal evaluability persists in the stated formal context; it does not select the operative or latent sibling and does not establish a different logical relation.
- `[1]`: Affirms that this internal formal evaluability persists independently of current operation, without asserting that a formal evaluation is occurring or that any result holds.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether this persistent internal formal evaluability claim holds; runtime uncertainty does not change the lexical root into operative or latent status.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** Lexical third trit `0` permanently selects the persistent status of this formal-evaluation affordance. Runtime `[2]/num` instead leaves the already-selected status claim unresolved; it never changes affordance status. Thus a latent root affirmed with `[1]` is a confirmed existing-but-nonoperative affordance, while a persistent or operative root carrying `[2]/num` remains lexically persistent or operative but uncertain as a token claim.
