# DAT:100–122 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges all nine provisional Data process meanings. It compares five genuinely different architectures per address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and routes 133 English concepts without forcing broad words into atoms.

## Packet-level design decision

The strongest architecture makes `DAT:1xx` the substrate-neutral process layer for represented information. The inward triplet transforms information while it remains within one reference entity, the outward triplet transfers bounded information from one source context to one distinct target, and the systemic triplet coordinates information-state alignment across multiple participants under one shared synchronization criterion. Completed, active, and nonactualized supply the exact third-trit contrast.

The three phases are mutually exclusive for the same bounded information process at one reference time. Lexical `1x2` means only that the typed process is not actualized; intention, planning, readiness, queueing, scheduling, future time, probability, and missing access remain separate. Runtime `[2]/num` remains uncertainty about the selected phase. The design rejects the provisional mixture of reads, writes, retrieval, assignment, aggregation, indexing, replication, successful outcome, access state, scheduling, and readiness. Information entities remain `DAT:0xx`; information-process affordances remain `DAT:2xx`; physical carriers, communication roles, cognition, formal evaluation, and creation remain other families.

### D. Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** completed internal data transformation

**Invariant:** Completion of a bounded transformation of represented information within one reference entity, yielding transformed information without transferring it to a distinct target.

**Inclusion boundary:** Includes only the completed internal data transformation as a substrate-neutral process affecting represented information at inward scope. The process transforms represented information within one entity and does not require a distinct target. The algorithm, input entity, output entity, and physical realization remain separate. It is neutral about physical medium, participant intent, semantic truth, success quality, persistence, access, observation, scheduling, probability, timing, and cause.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The datum, target reference, or shared dataset as an entity, and its persistent, manifest, or dormant status.
- The specific encoding, schema, algorithm, checksum, fidelity, consistency result, conflict policy, acknowledgment, or success criterion.
- Physical signal, medium, energy, spatial displacement, storage device, or material change realizing the information process.
- Communicative intent, sender or addressee role, message uptake, dialogue, social linkage, belief, understanding, attention, or observation.
- Creation of novel content, restoration to an acceptable prior state, structural reconfiguration, warning, fault, authority, or ownership.
- Capability, access, permission, intention, plan, readiness, queue, schedule, future time, possibility, necessity, probability, or cause.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_1`: Selects an information-state process rather than an information entity, physical carrier, participant interaction, cognitive act, or information-process affordance.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward/self scope: represented information transformed while retained within one reference entity and no other Data-family column.
- `third_trit`: Selects the completed phase of the same information process while leaving runtime truth status, quality, fidelity, acknowledgment, observer knowledge, tense, probability, and scheduling separate.

**Nearest neighbors:** `DAT:101`, `DAT:102`, `DAT:110`, `DAT:120`, `DAT:000`, `DAT:200`

**Same-family boundary:** `DAT:0xx` owns information entities and their persistent, manifest, or dormant status. `DAT:1xx` owns only transformations, target transfers, and systemic synchronizations of represented information. `DAT:2xx` remains reserved for information-process affordances or relations. A manifest datum does not entail a process, and a completed process does not entail persistence, fidelity, acknowledgment, or future accessibility. Completed, active, and nonactualized are mutually exclusive phases for the same bounded information process at one reference time. Lexical nonactualization states only that the typed process is not actualized; runtime `[2]/num` separately marks uncertainty about any selected phase.

**Runtime-state interpretation:**

- `[0]`: Denies that this internal data transformation completed; it does not assert that the active sibling is still running, that the nonactualized sibling holds, or that the result had either polarity.
- `[1]`: Affirms completion of this internal data transformation, without asserting success quality, fidelity, persistence, acknowledgment, physical transport, communication uptake, or later availability.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether this completed internal data transformation claim holds; runtime uncertainty does not change the lexical root into active or nonactualized phase.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** Lexical third trit `0` permanently selects the completed phase of this information process. Runtime `[2]/num` instead leaves the already-selected phase claim unresolved; it never changes process phase. Thus a nonactualized root affirmed with `[1]` is a confirmed typed-but-nonactualized data process, while a completed or active root carrying `[2]/num` remains lexically completed or active but uncertain as a token claim.
