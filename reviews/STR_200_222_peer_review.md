# STR:200–222 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges all nine provisional Structure capacity/relation meanings from first principles. It compares five genuinely different architectures at every address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and routes 82 English concepts without forcing broad words into atoms.

## Packet-level decision

The strongest architecture makes `STR:2xx` the affordance layer paired with canonical `STR:1xx`. `STR:200–202` says whether one structure can reassign its own constituents. `STR:210–212` says whether one distinct target is structurally qualified for incorporation into a preserved reference whole. `STR:220–222` says whether multiple structures are qualified to become peer constituents of a higher-order whole.

This replaces the Phase 2 mixture of load, adaptability, support, governance, coordination, and possible architecture. Those candidates were useful prompts but not one atomic grid: they crossed MAT, LOG, CON, REP, and CRE boundaries and did not align the three triplets. The recommended grid remains neutral about substrate, purpose, value, safety, and actual execution.

### Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** enduring internal reconfiguration capacity

**Invariant:** A persistent capacity of a reference structure to reassign its own constituents among its internal positions while preserving that structure as the reference whole.

**Boundary:** Includes only the persistent or established substrate-neutral structural affordance for internal reconfiguration capacity. The capacity concerns reassignment of the structure's own constituents; it neither adds a target nor forms a higher-order whole. It may be instantiated in physical, computational, organizational, or conceptual structures, but it does not assert that the matching STR:1xx transformation occurs, that any STR:0xx arrangement is realized, or that material, logical, informational, communicative, reparative, creative, temporal, or modal conditions hold.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The constituent position, interface, composite arrangement, constituent, target, or system as an entity or condition.
- An actual internal reconfiguration, target incorporation, or systemic integration process at any completion phase.
- Physical strength, load-bearing, elasticity, energy, contact, attachment force, containment, shelter, or material coupling.
- Set membership, type validity, formal compatibility, permission, authority, governance, schema conformance, or specification truth.
- Repairability, resilience, self-healing, creation, fabrication, deployment, communication, coordination, attention, or intention.
- Probability, readiness, plan, schedule, future time, necessity, unrestricted possibility, or guaranteed success.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_2`: Selects a structural capacity, qualifying relationship, or unrealized possibility rather than a locus/entity or an actual transformation.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward scope: the reference structure's own constituent organization; this root names the affordance for internal reconfiguration capacity and no competing family claim.
- `third_trit`: Selects persistent or established expression of the same internal reconfiguration capacity; runtime truth status, tense, probability, permission, and process phase remain separate.

**Nearest neighbors:** `STR:201`, `STR:202`, `STR:100`, `STR:101`, `STR:102`, `STR:000`

**Same-family boundary:** STR:0xx names constituent positions, structural interfaces, and composite arrangements. STR:1xx names actual transformations of constituent organization. STR:2xx names only the corresponding capacity or qualifying relation: inward reconfigurability, outward target incorporability, or systemic integrability. A capacity or relation does not entail that its process begins or completes; an actual process does not entail that the affordance persists afterward. Target incorporability preserves one reference whole and qualifies exactly one distinct target. Systemic integrability treats multiple inputs as peer candidates for a higher-order whole distinct from every input.

**Runtime meanings:**

- `[0]`: Denies that the enduring internal reconfiguration capacity holds in the stated structural context; it does not select the active or dormant sibling.
- `[1]`: Affirms that the enduring internal reconfiguration capacity holds, without asserting that any reconfiguration, incorporation, or integration process is occurring.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves the assertion that the enduring internal reconfiguration capacity holds pending, remembered, or unresolved; the lexical root remains the stable member.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** Lexical third trit 0 permanently selects the stable member of this affordance triplet. Runtime `[2]/num` instead marks a token-level assertion pending, remembered, or unresolved. Thus `STR:200[1]` affirms the lexical concept, while a stable or active sibling carrying `[2]/num` remains that sibling and does not become STR:200.
