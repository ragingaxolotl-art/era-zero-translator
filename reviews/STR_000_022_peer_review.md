# STR:000–022 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges all nine provisional Structure entity meanings. It compares five genuinely different architectures per address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and routes 91 English concepts without forcing broad words into atoms.

## Packet-level design decision

The strongest architecture makes `STR:0xx` the substrate-neutral entity/condition layer for structural loci and arrangements. The inward triplet names a constituent position, the outward triplet a boundary interface for engagement by a distinct target, and the systemic triplet a part–whole arrangement across multiple positions. Occupancy, engagement, and realization supply exact active/dormant contrasts.

The third-trit grid is deliberately not a three-way exclusive partition. Each `...0` member asserts persistence of structural identity and is neutral about present realization, so it may co-occur with either its `...1` or `...2` sibling. The `...1` and `...2` siblings are mutually exclusive for the same referent at the same reference time. Runtime `[2]/num` remains uncertainty about the selected claim rather than a lexical sibling.

The design rejects the provisional mixture of framework, mechanism, load-bearing, purpose, construction state, container, infrastructure, institution, roles, and coordination. Those require MAT, LIF, LOG, DAT, CRE, or later STR process/relation roots.

### D. Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** persistent constituent position

**Invariant:** A position within a structure that remains identified as a place for one constituent independently of its present occupancy.

**Inclusion boundary:** Includes only the persistent constituent position as a substrate-neutral claim about one constituent position internal to a reference structure. The claim is about the structural place available to or occupied by a constituent, not the constituent itself and not a spatial coordinate. It is neutral about substance, spatial coordinates, function, purpose, ownership, rules, construction, support relations, safety, communication, and social identity.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The constituent, physical component, material surface, body, building, container, person, institution, or organization as an independently identified entity.
- Spatial coordinate, destination, route, physical inside/outside region, movement, shape, mass, load, strength, or material integrity.
- Function, purpose, operation, authority, permission, role obligation, type constraint, compatibility, protocol, communication, or cognition.
- Blueprint, schema record, graph serialization, diagram, label, index, class declaration, or other information object describing structure.
- Building, assembling, rearranging, occupying, attaching, supporting, containing, repairing, or governing as a process or relation.
- Damage, failure, reservation, plan, schedule, probability, intended later use, or future realization—especially for third-trit 2.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_0`: Selects an entity or condition: persistent constituent position is a structural locus or arrangement, not a construction process, support relation, organizational capacity, material object, or information record.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward/self scope: one constituent position internal to a reference structure and no other structural column.
- `third_trit`: Selects the stable/persistent entity condition by occupancy, engagement, or structural realization while leaving runtime truth status and temporal scheduling separate.

**Nearest neighbors:** `STR:001`, `STR:002`, `STR:010`, `STR:020`, `STR:100`, `STR:200`

**Same-family boundary:** `STR:0xx` owns structural loci and part–whole arrangements as entities or conditions; `STR:1xx` owns structural change processes; `STR:2xx` remains reserved for structural capacities and continuing relations such as support, containment, dependency, fit, or systemic organization. Occupancy, engagement, or realization in 0xx does not itself name the process or relation that produced it. The persistent member is realization-neutral: it may co-occur with the occupied/engaged/instantiated sibling or with the vacant/unengaged/dormant sibling at the same reference time. It asserts continuity of structural identity, not a third mutually exclusive occupancy or realization value.

**Runtime-state interpretation:**

- `[0]`: Denies that the persistent constituent position remains identified in the stated structural context; it does not assert the active or dormant sibling.
- `[1]`: Affirms that the constituent position persists as identified across the stated context, without deciding present occupancy, engagement, realization, function, or integrity beyond the invariant.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether the persistent constituent position remains identified; it does not convert the lexeme into its active or dormant sibling.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** Lexical third trit `0` permanently selects the stable/persistent member of this structural triplet. Runtime `[2]/num` instead leaves the truth or applicability of that already-selected root unresolved; it never changes occupancy, engagement, or realization phase.
