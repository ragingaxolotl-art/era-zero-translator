# STR:100–122 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges all nine provisional Structure process meanings from first principles. It compares five genuinely different semantic architectures at every address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and routes 73 English concepts without forcing broad words into atoms.

## Packet-level decision

The strongest architecture makes `STR:1xx` a substrate-neutral structural-transformation grid. `STR:100–102` changes assignments of a structure's own constituents while preserving it as the reference whole. `STR:110–112` asymmetrically incorporates one distinct external target into an already identified reference that remains the organizing whole. `STR:120–122` makes two or more input structures peer constituents of a higher-order composite distinct from any single input. The third trit changes only completion phase.

The grid composes directly with canonical `STR:0xx`: internal reconfiguration changes occupancy of constituent positions, target incorporation brings a distinct target into a reference structure, and systemic integration organizes structures into a composite arrangement. It excludes construction, physical deformation, repair, planning, data migration, rules, communication, and ongoing structural relations.

### Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** completed internal reconfiguration

**Invariant:** Completion of a structural transformation that changes how a reference structure's own constituents occupy its internal positions.

**Boundary:** Includes only a completed substrate-neutral structural transformation that reassigns a reference structure's own constituents among its internal positions while preserving that structure as the reference whole. Changing the structure's position inventory itself, adding an outside target, or forming a higher-order whole requires a different structural claim. The transformation may be realized physically, computationally, institutionally, or conceptually, but it does not itself assert motion, material change, creation, repair, communication, rule change, data migration, temporal scheduling, function, safety, or success criteria beyond achieving the invariant result for a completed root.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The constituent position, interface, composite arrangement, constituent, target, material object, participant, or data record as an entity or condition.
- Spatial movement, route traversal, physical deformation, joining force, enclosure, fabrication, construction, destruction, or generation as such.
- Planning, intention, staging, queueing, scheduling, permission, readiness, probability, futurity, obligation, or specification—especially for lexical 1x2.
- Repair, restoration, reinforcement, stabilization, damage correction, success, fitness, integrity, function, or operational deployment.
- Communication, coordination, consensus, mental reclassification, schema-document editing, role authority, or institutional identity.
- Support, containment, attachment, dependency, fit, membership, compatibility, or other continuing relation after the transformation.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_1`: Selects a structural process, action, or transformation rather than a structural locus, entity condition, capacity, or relationship.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward/self scope: this root reassigns a reference structure's own constituents among its internal positions while preserving that structure as the reference whole; no competing family or temporal phase is added by this axis.
- `third_trit`: Selects the completed phase of the same internal-reconfiguration process; planning, queueing, schedule, futurity, permission, probability, and runtime truth status remain separate.

**Nearest neighbors:** `STR:101`, `STR:102`, `STR:110`, `STR:120`, `STR:000`, `STR:200`

**Same-family boundary:** STR:0xx owns constituent positions, structural interfaces, and composite arrangements as entities or conditions. STR:1xx owns changes in how constituents or structures participate in those arrangements. STR:2xx remains reserved for capacities and continuing relations such as support, containment, dependency, fit, attachment, or systemic compatibility. Target incorporation is asymmetric: the reference remains the whole and one external target becomes its constituent. Systemic integration is higher-order: every input structure becomes a co-constituent of a composite distinct from any one input. A completed STR:1xx root entails only the structural result stated in its invariant at the completion reference time; it does not entail persistence, function, integrity, or any STR:2xx relation unless separately asserted.

**Runtime meanings:**

- `[0]`: Denies that this internal-reconfiguration process reached completion in the stated structural context; it does not assert that the active or nonactualized sibling holds.
- `[1]`: Affirms that this internal-reconfiguration process reached its completed phase, without asserting success criteria, integrity, function, permanence, repair, or creation beyond the invariant.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether this internal-reconfiguration process reached completion or whether that completed result remains applicable; the lexical root stays completed.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** The lexical third trit permanently selects the completed member of this process triplet. Runtime `[2]/num` instead qualifies the truth, applicability, or resolution of a token of that already-selected root; it never changes the root into a planned, future, or different-phase process.
