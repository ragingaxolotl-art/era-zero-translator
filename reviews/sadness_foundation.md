# Unpleasant feeling before sadness

Author semantic review under the translator-completion goal. Reuse convention 11,
consciously felt self-condition, before specializing felt valence. None of the
324 root meanings changes. MIN representation, WRN harm and LIF care do not entail
unpleasant feeling; runtime 0 and negated happiness do not entail sadness.

Convention 18 adds unpleasant experiential tone to the same bound experiencer's
felt condition. It permits coexisting pleasant feeling and does not specify pain,
fear, anger, sadness, injury, disapproval or desire. Convention 19 reuses 18 and
adds sadness as sorrow or low spirits. It does not assert a diagnosis, loss,
crying, incapacity, permanence, lack of care or complete absence of happiness.
“Sad event,” object-directed sadness and figurative uses require separate frames.

The single-reference wrappers are compounds of compounds with exact version
dependencies and one preserved experiencer. The explicit convention, rather than
root adjacency, supplies the added meaning. There is no need to manufacture a new
root family or to append unrelated roots to satisfy a minimum graph size.

Acceptance requires sadness versus negated happiness, pleasant versus unpleasant
valence, participant changes, negation and all runtime states to remain distinct
in meaning and music. Existing compound definitions and performances must remain
unchanged. Author review and automated inverse checks are not independent listening
validation or evidence of complete emotional vocabulary.
