# WRN:200–222 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges all nine provisional Warning capacity/relation meanings from first principles. It compares five genuinely different architectures at every address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and routes 104 English concepts without forcing broad words into atoms.

## Packet-level decision

The strongest architecture makes `WRN:2xx` the adverse-affordance layer paired with canonical `WRN:1xx`. `WRN:200–202` says whether one reference entity is susceptible to internally originating degradation. `WRN:210–212` says whether one distinct hazard source and one target have an exposure relation that can enable impairment. `WRN:220–222` says whether one correlated adverse condition has a propagation pathway across multiple participants in a shared system.

This replaces the Phase 2 mixture of vulnerability, observer knowledge, current exploitation, uncertainty, cascading risk, and catastrophe possibility. Those candidates were useful prompts but blurred conditions, affordances, processes, evidence, probability, severity, and modality. The recommended grid uses one persistent/operative/latent contrast and remains neutral about substrate, observer knowledge, mechanics, likelihood, protective response, and actual realization.

**Invariant candidate:** A persistent susceptibility of one reference entity to undergo internally originating degradation under qualifying conditions, independently of whether that susceptibility is presently operative.

### Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** persistent internal susceptibility

**Invariant:** A persistent susceptibility of one reference entity to undergo internally originating degradation under qualifying conditions, independently of whether that susceptibility is presently operative.

**Boundary:** Includes only the persistent independently of current operation substrate-neutral adverse affordance for internal susceptibility. The susceptibility belongs to one reference entity and concerns internally originating degradation; a distinct hazard source selects the outward column. It may be realized physically, biologically, computationally, informationally, socially, or conceptually, but it does not assert that the matching WRN:1xx transformation occurs, that a WRN:0xx adverse condition manifests, or that mechanics, evidence, knowledge, severity, probability, control, recovery, temporal, or modal conditions hold.

**Excluded meanings:**

- The intrinsic fault, external hazard, distributed fault, bearer, source, target, participant, or system as an entity or adverse condition.
- An actual internal degradation, source-to-target impairment, or systemic propagation process at any completion phase.
- Physical force, energy, contact, permeability, route, biological mechanism, structural dependency, communicative channel, or data-flow edge as such.
- Detection, observability, diagnosis, evidence, warning messages, belief, confidence, attribution, severity, probability, expected loss, or risk score.
- Resistance, immunity, shielding, filtering, isolation, containment, blocking, repairability, recovery, or other protective affordance.
- Readiness, plan, schedule, future time, permission, necessity, unrestricted possibility, or guaranteed occurrence of harm.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_2`: Selects an adverse capacity or enabling relationship rather than an adverse-condition entity or an actual adverse transformation.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward scope: one reference entity and its internally originating adverse change; this root names the affordance for internal susceptibility and no competing family claim.
- `third_trit`: Selects persistent independently of current operation expression of the same internal susceptibility; runtime truth status, tense, probability, permission, and process phase remain separate.

**Nearest neighbors:** `WRN:201`, `WRN:202`, `WRN:100`, `WRN:101`, `WRN:102`, `WRN:000`

**Same-family boundary:** WRN:0xx names adverse-condition entities: intrinsic faults, distinct external hazards, and correlated distributed faults. WRN:1xx names actual adverse transformations: internal degradation, source-to-target impairment, and systemic propagation. WRN:2xx names only the corresponding adverse affordance: inward susceptibility, outward source-target exposure, or a systemic propagation pathway. An affordance does not entail that its process begins or completes; an actual process does not entail that the affordance persists afterward. Persistent 2x0 identity may co-occur with its operative or latent sibling, while operative and latent are mutually exclusive for the same affordance at one reference time.

**Runtime meanings:**

- `[0]`: Denies that the persistent internal susceptibility persists in the stated warning context; it does not select the operative or latent sibling and does not assert safety.
- `[1]`: Affirms that the persistent internal susceptibility persists independently of current operation, without asserting that degradation, impairment, or propagation is occurring.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether the persistent internal susceptibility persists; the lexical root remains the persistent member and does not become latent.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** Lexical third trit 0 permanently selects the persistent member of this adverse-affordance triplet. Runtime `[2]/num` instead marks a token-level assertion pending, remembered, or unresolved. Thus `WRN:200[1]` affirms the lexical concept, while a persistent or operative sibling carrying `[2]/num` remains that sibling and never becomes a latent root.
