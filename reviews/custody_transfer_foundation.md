# Transfer of physical custody

Status: delegated author review under the user-directed completion goal, not
independent linguistic validation. No atom or existing compound is changed.

This foundation reuses custody version 1.0.0 as the source pre-state and
acquisition version 1.0.0 as the recipient transition. Convention 9 explicitly
adds their linkage as one reallocation, with source loss at culmination. Mere
conjunction would allow unrelated loss and pickup, or different objects. Two
slot paths must bind the same object; the two holders must be distinct, and each
retains the inherited distinction from the object. The semantic head is the exact
acquisition compound, so its actualization ending remains the outer runtime.

The selected sense is a **relinquishing transfer of practical physical custody**.
The source has control immediately before and lacks it at culmination; the
recipient lacks it before and has it at culmination. Neither source control nor
the acquired relation is independently asserted at speech time. A source who
retains shared custody does not satisfy this sense, even though another holder
may acquire custody. Third parties can retain custody. Neither willingness nor
lawful authority is required. Ownership, carrying, spatial displacement and
persistence afterward are unspecified. English “give” and “deliver” have wider
or different commitments and are not automatically aliases of this compound.

The graph's source custody is a contextual pre-state, not an assertion made true
by setting runtime 0. Runtime 0, 1 and 2 still characterize the whole occurrence
independently; lexical addresses, negation, tense and event completion are separate.

No new LIF/STR-like family is justified. DAT:110 transfers information, CON:110
receives communicative expression, MOV:110 arrives spatially, and MAT:210 alone
supplies physical coupling. None already means custody reallocation. Convention
9 is an explicitly audible lexical commitment, reusing the reviewed relation and
event instead of redefining any of those roots.

## Acceptance distinctions

- A box arrives beside Ada while Ben retains control: no transfer.
- Ada acquires an unattended object: acquisition without a source transfer.
- Ada gains shared control while Ben retains custody: not this transfer sense.
- Ben loses control, then Ada independently finds the box: event linkage absent.
- Ben's box leaves his custody while Ada gains a different box: object mismatch.
- A borrowed box passes from Ben's control to Ada's: ownership need not change.
- The same object remains stationary while control of its enclosure passes: no
  movement or carrying is required.

Implementation uses `CMP:e3075c904daf1c647969fef6`, version 1.0.0. Past positive
and negative statements, questions, commands and selected heavy/light restrictions
bind source, object and recipient explicitly. The English parser's role mapping
uses complete nested slot paths. Bare controlled `ROLE source` is deliberately
ambiguous between the two nested custody holders; it must not silently bind both.
The exact semantic IR supplies scoped bindings. The local controlled compiler
now accepts explicit JSON-array SLOT paths and backward REF paths; see
[scoped controlled slots](controlled_scoped_slots.md).

The MIDI inverse reads no source IR or expected participants. It checks both
custody structures, the acquisition structure, their exact roots, shared audible
object reference and the outer convention. It recovers the distinct pre/post
roles and all runtime states. The sound alone does not resolve homophones into
external identities or establish that two identically sounding names differ.

event. Neither spatial carrying nor transfer by itself supplies delivery. All
