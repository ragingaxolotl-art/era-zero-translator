# Contextual standards without fabricated measurements

## User-facing gap

The explicit numeric standard routes make a selected physical reading precise,
but requiring a number for every ordinary adjective is narrower than the goal.
“The lamp is bright” does not assert that the speaker measured a luminance value.
Likewise, “the radio is hot” can invoke a contextual thermal standard without
specifying it numerically. A numeric threshold is an optional clarification, not
the only possible semantic representation of a contextual comparison.

## Required semantic distinction

Retain a typed reference to an unresolved contextual degree standard: the property
dimension, comparison direction and contextual norm must be explicit, while the
numeric value remains unspecified. Do not fill it with a default measurement or
encode “unknown” as runtime 2. Occurrence runtime and an uninstantiated contextual
parameter are independent. The contextual standard should be audible and distinct
from a concrete measured bound and from comparison against another named bearer.

Hot/cold and bright/dim need their own contextual norms. Do not infer that being
not hot means being cold, that hot and cold share a threshold, or that a contextual
adjective supplies an exact quantity. Scope and negation must apply to the whole
comparison. A supplied numeric standard may instantiate the context deliberately;
the translation should retain that selection as provenance.

## Dependency order and acceptance

1. Add a typed contextual-standard reference and validate its attachment to a
   physical property comparison. Begin with the already reviewed mass, temperature
   and luminance dimensions; invent no new root meanings.
2. Add a distinct musical encoding and independent inverse. Preserve all prior
   concrete measurement and entity-comparison performances.
3. Let users select the appropriate physical sense with either a contextual
   standard or a numeric one. Do not silently equate visual brightness with
   luminance, weight burden with mass, or subjective warmth with temperature.
4. Test negated versus opposite adjectives, changed bearers, shared versus distinct
   standards, temporal/modal scope, 0/1/2, and contextual-versus-numeric contrasts.

Perceptual brightness, tactile roughness, rate/manner and metaphorical senses need
separate reviewed dimensions or meanings. This standard-reference grammar is not
permission to label every adjective with an arbitrary physical unit. Existing
numeric implementations remain useful, but do not establish unrestricted adjective
translation or completion of the original phrase diagnostic.
