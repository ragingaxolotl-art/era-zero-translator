# LOG:000–022 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW EVIDENCE — CANONICAL APPROVAL RECORDED SEPARATELY**

This packet challenges all nine provisional Logic entity meanings. It compares five genuinely different architectures per address, scores 45 candidates across 360 criterion values, and routes 126 English concepts without forcing broad words into atoms.

## Packet-level design decision

The strongest architecture makes `LOG:0xx` the substrate-neutral entity layer for formal constraints. The inward triplet identifies one criterion within a reference entity's logical specification, the outward triplet identifies one formal constraint assigned to a distinct target, and the systemic triplet identifies one correlated formal regime across multiple participants in a shared rule domain. Persistence, present operation, and dormancy supply the exact third-trit contrast.

Each `...0` member asserts persistence of rule identity and is neutral about present operation, so it may co-occur with either its `...1` or `...2` sibling. The operative `...1` and dormant `...2` siblings are mutually exclusive for the same rule at the same reference time. Runtime `[2]/num` remains uncertainty about the selected claim. The design rejects the provisional mixture of axioms, propositions, variable bindings, operands, values, executing programs, and dormant procedures. Evaluation and inference remain for `LOG:1xx`, formal applicability and consequence relations for `LOG:2xx`, and represented values and specifications for DAT.

### D. Recommended permanent semantic specification

**Title:** persistent internal formal criterion

**Invariant:** A persistent formal criterion within the logical specification of one reference entity that limits that entity's admissible states independently of whether the criterion is presently operative.

**Inclusion boundary:** Includes only the persistent internal formal criterion as a substrate-neutral formal constraint at inward scope. The claim is about a criterion internal to one reference specification, not a rule assigned to another target, a proposition's truth, or a stored record. It is neutral about truth, satisfaction, violation, evaluation, enforcement, represented form, belief, physical realization, authority, probability, timing, and cause.

**Excluded meanings:**

- Truth or falsity of a proposition, or assertion, belief, certainty, and evidential support for it.
- Satisfaction, violation, validity, invalidity, proof, derivation, inference, evaluation, or execution result.
- Data value, variable, binding, symbol string, record, schema representation, source code, or storage state.
- Physical, biological, communicative, structural, restorative, or adverse mechanism governed by or realizing the rule.
- Authorship, institutional authority, command, enforcement, sanction, social acceptance, or responsibility.
- Applicability, entailment, consistency, computability, possibility, necessity, probability, schedule, future activation, or cause.

**Address justification:**

- `first_trit_0`: Selects a formal rule or constraint entity rather than an evaluation process, proof act, execution, or formal applicability/consequence relation.
- `second_trit`: Selects inward/self scope: one formal criterion within the logical specification of one reference entity and no other Logic-family column.
- `third_trit`: Selects the persistent/operation-neutral member by rule identity or present operation while leaving runtime truth status, satisfaction, observer knowledge, tense, probability, and process phase separate.

**Nearest neighbors:** `LOG:001`, `LOG:002`, `LOG:010`, `LOG:020`, `LOG:100`, `LOG:200`

**Same-family boundary:** `LOG:0xx` owns formal constraint entities; `LOG:1xx` remains the layer for actual evaluation, inference, or rule-application processes; `LOG:2xx` remains the layer for applicability, consequence, consistency, or other formal enabling relations. Present operation in 0xx does not name an evaluation or entail satisfaction, and an identified rule does not entail that it can be applied or that a result follows. The persistent member is operation-neutral: it may co-occur with the operative sibling or the dormant sibling at the same reference time. It asserts continuity of rule identity rather than a third mutually exclusive operation value.

**Runtime-state interpretation:**

- `[0]`: Denies that this internal formal criterion persists as the same identified formal rule; it does not select the operative or dormant sibling and does not prove an opposite rule.
- `[1]`: Affirms the persistent identity of this internal formal criterion while leaving current operation, satisfaction, truth, enforcement, representation, and origin undecided.
- `[2/num]`: Leaves unresolved whether this persistent internal formal criterion remains identified; uncertainty about the assertion does not change the lexical root into an operative or dormant criterion.

**Lexical/runtime distinction:** Lexical third trit `0` permanently selects the persistent/operation-neutral member of this formal-constraint triplet. Runtime `[2]/num` instead leaves the truth or applicability of that already-selected claim unresolved; it never changes operation status. Thus a dormant root affirmed with `[1]` is a confirmed defined-but-inoperative rule, while a persistent or operative root carrying `[2]/num` remains lexically persistent or operative but uncertain as a token claim.
