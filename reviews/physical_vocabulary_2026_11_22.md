These entries follow the existing unbound story-compound architecture, rather than adding incompatible internal role slots. They must not be reused to assert separate participants or independent times for their nested phases.

Names and abstract targets require a focused sense question. “Release the update,” “drop the subject,” “the price fell,” “an empty promise,” and “I am full” must not silently acquire these physical meanings.
