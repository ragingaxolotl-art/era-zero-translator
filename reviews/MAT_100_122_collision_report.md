# MAT:100–122 Collision Report

Status: **peer-review evidence — not canonical**

## Core ownership rule

The proposed block owns intrinsic physical-state change. It does not own positional displacement, participant transfer, structure, purpose, damage, repair, living growth, data mutation, control flow, or ontological becoming. Locus and lexical phase are fixed by address; change kind, cause, magnitude, result, and evaluation remain compositional.

## Intra-Matter boundary

| Layer | Owns | Must not absorb |
|---|---|---|
| `MAT:0xx` | material entities and conditions after their own review | process events |
| `MAT:1xx` proposed | physical-state change by locus and phase | material capacity, stored energy, force relation, conservation law |
| `MAT:2xx` pending | capacities and relationships after later review | entity or process semantics already assigned elsewhere |

## High-risk English traps

- **Change** is not automatically Matter; select the family of the changed state.
- **Move, carry, circulate, inject, emit, and transport** foreground displacement or participant relations and require MOV/CON even when matter participates.
- **Build, fabricate, manufacture, and create** require CRE/STR purpose and organization; MAT supplies only physical realization when present.
- **Break, corrode, decay, and destroy** require WRN or other evaluation when damage or loss is asserted.
- **Repair and heal** require REP; physical change alone does not encode restoration.
- **Queued, staged, scheduled, ready, likely, and future** are never lexical meanings of third trit 2.
- **Completed** describes the process boundary, not a stable, successful, intact, useful, or known result.

## Disposition

All nine recommendations stand alone as Matter process atoms, modify or combine with other-family predicates when those semantics are present, and avoid blocking collisions. They remain provisional pending explicit user approval.
