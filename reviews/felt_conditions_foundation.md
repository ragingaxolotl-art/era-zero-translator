# Felt conditions before ordinary affect and bodily adjectives

Status: author review under the completion goal. This is an explicit
conventional extension, not a claim that adjacency of existing roots entails
subjective experience. No root family, atomic invariant, musical address or prior
compound changes. The prior foundation-gap and perception reviews identify the
missing distinction across the 324-root inventory. The subsequent support,
carrying and custody compounds do not add felt experience either.

MIN:001 supplies a manifest mental presentation of the cognizer's own condition;
LIF:001 supplies presently conscious selfhood. Convention 11 binds their focal
self to the explicit experiencer and adds that the presentation is consciously
felt. A stored self-model, unconscious registration or a device log is insufficient.
Implicit feeling need not be a verbal judgment or reflective description. The
focal self is the bound experiencer, so “Eli is hungry” does not turn into a claim
about the speaker. This framing is explicit in the audible convention rather than
an unmarked shift in what an existing self root means.

Dependency order:

1. **11: consciously felt self-condition**, with one experiencer slot.
2. **12: pleasant felt condition**, reusing 11 and adding pleasant valence.
3. **13: happy felt mood**, reusing 12 and selecting gladness/contentment rather
   than every pleasant sensory quality or favorable judgment.
4. **14: felt bodily replenishment need**, reusing 11 and qualifying it with
   MAT:000, explicitly the same experiencer's own material body.
5. **15: felt hunger** and **16: felt thirst**, reusing 14 and distinguishing
   food-directed and water-directed bodily demand.
6. **17: felt fatigue**, reusing 11; weariness of physical or mental effort,
   without asserting dormancy, objective incapacity or damage.

The specialized one-reference wrappers are intentional compounds of compounds.
Their additional lexical commitments have distinct convention sounds and exact
version dependencies. Adding an unrelated root just to make a larger graph would
introduce an unwarranted claim. These definitions do not justify a new 27-root
family or assign feelings to the three runtime endings.

## Counterexamples and scope

Care can occur without pleasant feeling. Happiness is not mere care, attention,
fortune, willingness or a favorable evaluation. A happy mood may coexist with
sadness or discomfort. Hunger and thirst describe felt bodily demands, not proven
laboratory deficits: neither entails harm, eating or drinking. Fatigue is not
sleep or loss of consciousness and need not make effort impossible. An unfelt
nutritional deficit is not this felt-hunger predicate. Figurative hunger for
success, thirst for knowledge and being tired of an object require other senses.

Each nested reference retains the same experiencer. The outer predicate has its
own runtime 0/1/2; the slot can independently contain a lexical participant with
another runtime. Negation and past/future scope stay outside the felt predicate.
“Was hungry” must not become dormant hunger, and a negative hunger assertion must
not be confused with a positive assertion carrying runtime 0.

The implementation checks 96 ordinary positive/negative statements, questions,
past and explicit future readings with novel experiencers and all outer runtime
states, plus 21 controlled dependency cases with independent participant states.
Exact prior definitions and MIDI-only semantic recovery are checked; corrupted
root music is rejected. These authored checks are not held-out validation or medical diagnosis.
Broader emotion, desire/preference, sensory qualities and movement manner remain
separate requirements; no alias silently substitutes this batch for them.
