# Independent destination-carrying MIDI inverse

`pages/carrying_decoder.mjs` accepts strict MIDI bytes and the fixed root sound
inventory. It receives no source sentence, semantic graph, formal events,
expected digest or compound rendering plan. The existing strict observer first
recovers audible tokens; the new interpreter validates the complete nested
profile for conventions 6/5/4/3 or 5/4/3. It does not accept a convention marker
alone as proof that the expected compound was performed.

The inverse recovers the ongoing destination-directed or completed carrying event
type; carrier, load and spatial destination; outer runtime 0/1/2; speech-act
contour; and ordered negation, modality, confidence, aspect and supported time
scopes. References retain their phonemes, instance code, nominal mode or deictic
role. Lexical participants retain their own root and runtime. A definite noun's
mass restriction retains its comparison and exact kilogram threshold.

Every nested root, role, reference, qualification and convention must occur in
the released structure. Repeated load and destination references must agree,
including their properties. Inner process roots carry no independent runtime
ending; the outer head has exactly one. A valid but different root, unknown
convention, recipient role or inconsistent destination is rejected. Unsupported
clauses, predicates and additional tokens are rejected as a whole, without a
partial semantic result.

The returned predicate is inside the ordered scopes. A completed event type
under negation, modality, ongoing or habitual scope is not an assertion that an
arrival happened now. The return value records event-type commitments separately
from those operators; it never converts the runtime code into tense or polarity.

## Evidence and limits

The inverse suite uses 36 English/state performances with independently written
expected participants, property values and scope order. Six additional local
Ruby controlled-syntax performances test lexical arguments with independent
0/1/2 states. Octave transposition preserves the recovered meaning. Raw MIDI
mutations remain grammatically balanced under the strict observer but fail this
semantic profile when they alter a convention, role, repeated destination, root
or one repeated mass threshold. Truncation and unsupported multi-clause input
also fail. Role reversal is recovered, not collapsed.

This is a bounded semantic projection, not the original IR serialization or a
general semantic inverse. Same-reference sounds are consistency evidence; the
learned conventions assert participant sharing. They do not prove external
identity, distinguish untagged homophones, or recover English spelling. A Kay/Cay
contrast explicitly demonstrates that limit. The same-event linkage is part of
the reviewed convention's meaning, not inferred from timing alone.

The profile currently excludes non-destination carrying, external “toward”
attachments, arbitrary compound participants, quantifiers, conditions, multiple
clauses and general discourse. It does not establish perception of arbitrary
recorded audio, human listening accuracy, broad English coverage or completion
of the original gates. No root, compound definition or performance encoding is
changed by adding this decoder.
