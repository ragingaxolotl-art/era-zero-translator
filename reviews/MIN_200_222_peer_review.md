# MIN:200–222 Adversarial Semantic Design Review

Status: **REVIEW COMPLETE — RECOMMENDED PENDING INDEPENDENT VALIDATION AND DELEGATED APPROVAL**

This packet reviews only `MIN:200–222`. It treats every Phase 2 meaning as replaceable, preserves all deterministic generated fields, leaves the 99 existing canonical meanings unchanged, and does not begin `CON:000–022`, Gate 4, compounds, or translator implementation.

## Block conclusion

The strongest permanent architecture is **selective allocation of cognitive processing**, conventionally glossed as attention. `MIN:200–202` owns the cognizer's attention-allocation capacity; `MIN:210–212` owns the relation between a cognizer's preferential processing and one target; `MIN:220–222` owns joint attention across multiple cognizers. This fills the attention territory deliberately excluded from canonical `MIN:000–122`.

The current Phase 2 grid is rejected because competence, focus, aptitude, belief, uncertainty, worldview, distributed intelligence, and hypothesis space do not share one semantic invariant. Several entries also collide with LOG, CON, DAT, CRE, or ORI.

## Candidate architecture comparison

| Architecture | Strength | Permanent limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Current Phase 2 mixture | Familiar cognitive vocabulary | No invariant or 3×3 symmetry; major cross-family collisions |
| Attention allocation | Exact capacity/target/systemic mapping; everyday and technical utility | Requires compounds for perception, learning, goals, and implementation |
| Representational grounding | Strong technical semantics and continuity with MIN:0xx | Risks duplicating representation content and aboutness |
| Epistemic stance | Covers belief, doubt, and agreement | Collides with LOG, ORI, and CON; excludes nonpropositional attention |
| Executive control | Covers regulation and coordinated agency | Collides with CRE goals, STR control, and CON coordination |
