## `LIF:000` — continuous self

**Compact root:** `nam`  
**Full carrier:** `ni-ma-ma-ma`

**Meaning:**

> The self understood as a continuous identity across time and changing states.

This is deeper than English “I.” It means the identity that remains the same even if:

- the body changes
- memories are added
- the individual sleeps
- the individual enters `num`
- the individual is transferred into another structure

### Trit structure

- `0`: entity or condition
- `0`: inward/self
- `0`: stable and continuous

### C-reference melody

```text
Opening: C4–E4–G4
Address: C4–C4–C4–C4
```

Because all three trits are `0`, the pitch remains unchanged.

### Example

```text
LIF:000 [1]
```

> “I exist as the same continuous self.”

Spoken compactly:

> **Nam.**

## `LIF:002` — dormant self

**Compact root:** `nan`  
**Full carrier:** `ni-ma-ma-nu`

**Meaning:**

> The self preserved without present conscious expression.

This may refer to:

- sleep
- suspended awareness
- a saved identity
- a backed-up consciousness
- an individual in `num`
- an identity awaiting embodiment

It does **not** mean dead or destroyed.

### Trit structure

- `0`: entity
- `0`: inward/self
- `2`: dormant, unresolved, or potential

### C-reference melody

```text
Opening: C4–E4–G4
Address: C4–C4–C4–B♭3
```

### Important distinction

```text
LIF:002 [1]
```

means:

> “A dormant self currently and validly exists.”

But:

```text
LIF:002 [0]
```

means:

> “The dormant self is unavailable, invalid, or no longer exists.”

The root describes the **kind of identity**. The ending describes its **current reality-state**.
