# Reusable copying and mental retrieval — November 22

The translator now composes **copy/copies/copied/copying** and the retrieval sense of **recall/remember** across its supported sentence grammar. These are two explicit conventional compounds using existing roots. The dictionary contains 324 unchanged roots, 117 compounds and 578 indexed senses. Fingerprint tests protect all earlier compound and sense records. The [semantic review](../reviews/information_vocabulary_2026_11_22.md) explains the dependencies, phase bindings, rejected substitutions and approval provenance.

Copy creates a distinct retained information object with equivalent relevant content under one contextual criterion. Its source remains distinct from the result and is not removed by this operation. Mere transfer, moving and bitwise identity are not silently substituted. Recall brings a previously latent mental representation into manifest cognition, reusing the existing reviewed meaning of remember. It does not assert truth, new learning or persistent memory alone. The latter words ask a focused sense question; choosing another meaning leaves the sentence unresolved.

Commands now use the same complete object parser as statements. This repairs a general article-only restriction that rejected names and pronouns. **Recall Mira**, **Push Mira**, **Delete Mira** with an explicit information-item interpretation, **Find Nora**, **Keep the gate open** and **Copy files** are checked. A person named **Copy** remains the subject in **Copy dropped the box**. This is shared grammar repair, not a special rule for one example.
