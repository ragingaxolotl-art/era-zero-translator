# Contextual physical width — November 22

**Wide and narrow now describe a physical bearer’s width relative to a local standard.** They extend the existing contextual adjective grammar, reusing its participant, scope and music structure. No new root or compound is introduced. The dictionary has 324 roots, 117 compounds and 589 indexed senses: ten new discovery routes cover the eight existing physical adjectives and wide/narrow.

The user chooses **Use physical width** before translating. This describes the object’s transverse extent, not the clearance through an opening, apparent size or figurative breadth. Wide and narrow have separate local standards; neither means the negation of the other. See the [meaning review](../reviews/contextual_width_2026_11_22.md). Earlier roots, compounds, senses and the eight earlier adjective sound codes remain unchanged. Norm codes 8 and 9 extend the existing profile.
