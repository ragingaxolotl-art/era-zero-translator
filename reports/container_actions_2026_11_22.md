# Reusable filling and emptying actions

**Fill** and the action **empty** now work with different actors and physical containers, including statements, questions, commands, conditions, pronouns and ordered scopes. **Nora filled the bottle** and **Mira emptied the box** use distinct action meanings. The existing **empty/full** adjectives retain their definitions, identifiers and sounds.

The two new compounds reuse approved caused displacement, full/empty states and actualization. The moved participants are the contents; the outer target is the container. Filling reaches a fixed contextual capacity threshold from below; emptying removes all relevant contents from a previously nonempty container. Ongoing aspect does not assert completion. Runtime 0/1/2 remains separate from time, amount and negation. No new root is needed. The [semantic review](../reviews/container_actions_2026_11_22.md) records dependencies and exclusions; it is an author review, not independent linguistic approval.

## Phase and coverage boundaries

**Nora filled the empty box** needs to distinguish the initial empty state from the result. The current modifier representation does not encode that phase, so this sentence receives a specific explanation and no whole-sentence score. **The box was empty before Nora filled it** explicitly preserves the two events, their order and the same container. Nested private adjectives containing empty/full receive the same phase check; their saved definitions and sounds are preserved.

**Fill the bottle with water**, **empty the box into the bin**, quantities, phrasal variants, passive and perfect forms remain unsupported. Other meanings of fill/empty require a focused choice and are not converted to the physical action automatically. General recorded-audio recovery remains unfinished; these are strict MIDI checks, with no original English supplied to the decoder.
