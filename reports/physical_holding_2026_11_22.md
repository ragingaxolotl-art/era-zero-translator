# Reusable physical holding — November 22

**Hold, holds, held and holding** now use the existing physical holding foundation `CMP:834a56d90e31b430990720ff`, sound code 76. Release and drop already depend on that exact meaning. This adds one English verb sense and a shared sentence frame, with no new root, compound or musical code. There are 324 roots, 117 compounds and 579 indexed senses.

The actor is the holder and the target is the held object. Holding maintains a grip, restraint or load-bearing engagement; it need not mean ownership, exclusive control, intention, being stationary or carrying the object's entire weight. Held places the relation in the past without inventing an acquisition or release. Figurative objects and ambiguous alternatives ask for a physical interpretation. The [review](../reviews/physical_holding_surface_2026_11_22.md) records those commitments and exclusions.
